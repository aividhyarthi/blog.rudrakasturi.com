## Editorial and visual checklist

- Each article has a clear headline, one-sentence description and two or three sentence summary.
- Each article explains what happened, why it matters and what readers should do.
- Each article includes reader FAQs and at least two internal links.
- Primary source URLs and review dates are in front matter.
- Each article has an original 1600 × 900 SVG illustration in `public/images/editorial/`.
- The official source is credited directly below the original illustration. The draft does not embed unverified third-party image URLs. Add an official-source image only after its actual image URL and reuse terms have been checked.
- Vendor benchmarks and arena scores are attributed to the vendor.
- Drafts remain unpublished until approved.
