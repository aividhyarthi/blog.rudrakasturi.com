_Based on research by [GMBapi.com](https://gmbapi.com/news/google-deletes-5-star-reviews/)_

**Have you ever noticed that some of your 5-star reviews disappeared from your Google Business Profile?**

You’re not alone. Google has been cracking down on fake, spammy, and suspicious reviews to keep its platform fair and trustworthy. But sometimes, even **real reviews** get removed, leaving business owners frustrated.

So, **why does this happen**? Let’s break down what we’ve learned from a major study by [Michel](https://gmbapi.com/author/michel/) from **[GMBapi.com](https://news.seofomo.co/story/how-and-why-google-is-deleting-reviews/)**, where they analyzed nearly **5 million reviews** across **78 countries** and **19,000 business locations**. Their research uncovered surprising patterns about how and why Google deletes reviews — and what businesses can do to avoid it.

### **Why Is Google Deleting Reviews?**

Google wants to make sure reviews are **genuine and trustworthy**. If a review seems suspicious, fake, or violates their guidelines, it’s likely to be removed.

Here are some common reasons why Google deletes reviews:

*   **Fake or Paid Reviews:** If reviews seem bought or incentivized, they’re gone.
*   **Spam or Offensive Language:** Reviews with bad language or personal attacks are removed.
*   **Conflict of Interest:** Reviews from employees or business owners get deleted.
*   **Keyword Overuse:** Reviews that seem too generic or repetitive can be flagged.

### **Surprising Insights from GMBapi.com’s Study**

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/image-60-1024x827.png)

#### **1\. Google Targets 5-Star Reviews the Most!**

👉 **69.5% of deleted reviews** were **5-star ratings!**

**Why?**

*   Google often suspects 5-star reviews of being **fake or paid for**, especially if they sound too good to be true.
*   Phrases like **“Great service,” “Excellent experience,” or “Good staff”** are considered generic and can raise red flags.

**What About 1- or 2-Star Reviews?**

*   **Lower ratings (1-2 stars)** are less likely to be deleted, **unless** they have offensive or spammy content.

#### **2\. Review Deletions Happen in Waves**

Google doesn’t delete reviews every day. The study found that deletions happen **in waves**, especially after major Google updates.

**Key Dates When Reviews Were Deleted:**

*   **September 2024:** Google’s **Helpful Content Update** cracked down on fake reviews.
*   **October 2024:** The **Spam Update** targeted spammy content, including reviews.
*   **November-December 2024:** The **November Core Update** focused on improving content quality, leading to more deletions.

#### **3\. Best Days to Avoid Review Deletions?**

According to GMBapi.com’s research, **Wednesdays and Fridays** were the busiest days for review deletions. **Sundays** had the fewest deletions — maybe Google’s team takes Sundays off!

### **Does Replying to Reviews Help?**

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/image-59-1024x602.png)

**Yes!** Businesses that respond to reviews seem to have fewer reviews deleted.

*   **73.7% of deleted reviews** were from businesses that **didn’t respond** at all.
*   **Only 26.3% of deleted reviews** were from businesses that **did reply.**

**Why Does This Matter?**  
Google likely sees businesses that **engage with their customers** as **more credible and transparent**, reducing the chance of reviews being removed.

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/image-61-1024x737.png)

### **How Long a Review Is Matters, Too**

*   **Short Reviews:** If reviews are too short or generic, they are more likely to be deleted.
*   **Longer Reviews:** Unhappy customers tend to write long, detailed reviews — and these are less likely to be flagged.

## **What Businesses Can Do to Keep Their Reviews Safe**

So, how can you keep your reviews safe from deletion? Here’s a simple action plan:

### **1\. Avoid Paid or Fake Reviews**

🚫 Never buy reviews or ask friends, family, or employees to leave reviews. Google can spot this easily!

### **2\. Ask Real Customers for Reviews**

✅ Encourage real customers to leave **honest feedback** after their experience. Use follow-up emails or SMS reminders if needed.

### **3\. Respond to Reviews (Even the Good Ones!)**

💬 Reply to all reviews — positive and negative. Thank customers for good reviews and address issues from bad reviews. Google notices businesses that **engage**.

### **4\. Watch for Policy Violations**

🔎 Read Google’s Review Guidelines. Avoid reviews with spammy language, personal attacks, or conflicts of interest.

### **5\. Monitor and Report Fake Reviews**

⚠️ Use the Google My Business dashboard to **report fake or malicious reviews**. This helps keep your profile clean and trustworthy.

## **What Not to Do (Important!)**

*   **Don’t Use Review Generators:** Stay away from third-party review services.
*   **Don’t Write Reviews for Your Own Business:** Google will spot this quickly.
*   **Don’t Ignore Bad Reviews:** Unreplied negative reviews can hurt your credibility.
