Ask ChatGPT to recommend a project management tool for a small business. It may mention Asana, Trello, Monday.com or ClickUp. Ask Perplexity the same question and you may receive a different list.

For a brand, this is becoming an important visibility question. A company may rank well on Google for its product category but still be missing when a customer asks an AI assistant for recommendations.

So how do ChatGPT and Perplexity decide which brands to mention?

The honest answer is that neither company publishes a complete formula for brand recommendations. Their systems can search, retrieve and synthesise information, but the exact selection process is not fully public. We can still examine the information available to them and build a practical approach to improving brand discoverability.

## Why does an AI platform mention one brand and not another?

A recommendation question is different from a definition question.

If someone asks "What is project management software?", the answer can explain the category without naming many brands.

If the question is "Which project management software is suitable for a five-member marketing agency?", the answer needs to identify products that appear relevant to that requirement.

The platform needs information about the category, the products, their features and the user's stated needs. It may retrieve information from different websites before preparing the answer.

A brand's absence from one answer does not automatically mean that the brand is weak or that a competitor has paid for placement.

## Five areas brands should examine

### Is the brand clearly identifiable online?

A platform needs to understand which organisation or product a name refers to.

This can become difficult when:

- Several companies use similar names.
- The company has changed its name.
- The product has a different name from its parent company.
- The website provides little information about the organisation.
- Different websites describe the same product in conflicting ways.

Create a consistent source of information about the company.

The official website should clearly explain the brand name, products, services, location, ownership where relevant and contact details. Important company information should not exist only inside images or promotional videos.

### Can the platform find useful information about the product?

A brand homepage saying "We are India's leading technology company" does not explain what the product does.

A useful product page should answer practical questions:

- What problem does the product solve?
- Who is it designed for?
- What are its main features?
- What does it cost?
- What are its limitations?
- How does it compare with alternatives?
- Where can a customer obtain support?

These details help customers make decisions. They also provide more useful information for search systems to discover and interpret.

Do not publish unsupported claims such as "the best product in India" without explaining the evidence behind them.

### Is the brand discussed by credible external sources?

A company's own website is important, but it is not the only place where information about the company exists.

Customers may encounter a brand through:

- Independent product reviews.
- Industry publications.
- Research reports.
- Professional communities.
- Customer discussions.
- Government or regulatory records.
- Partner websites.

The value of these sources depends on their relevance and reliability. A hundred low-quality mentions are not automatically more useful than one detailed, credible review.

Brands should focus on genuine product coverage, customer experience and accurate third-party information rather than buying artificial mentions.

### Does the content answer the actual recommendation question?

A product page may describe every feature but fail to explain who should use the product.

Consider a software company that sells accounting tools.

It could create useful pages addressing:

- Accounting software for small businesses.
- Accounting software for freelancers.
- Accounting software for businesses with multiple branches.
- Accounting software with GST invoicing.
- Accounting software for first-time business owners.

Each page should provide real differences and decision-making information. Changing only the heading while repeating the same generic product description does not create meaningful coverage.

### Can the platform access the website?

A brand may have useful content that a crawler cannot access.

Check whether important pages return the correct HTTP status, load their main content, expose crawlable links and avoid accidental blocking.

OpenAI's guidance for ChatGPT Search explains that websites should allow OAI-SearchBot to crawl them if they want to be eligible for inclusion in search results. It also notes that placement is not guaranteed.

Eligibility is not the same as selection. Allowing a crawler to access a website does not guarantee that the brand will be mentioned.

## How to check whether ChatGPT and Perplexity mention your brand

Create a repeatable brand visibility test.

Do not ask only "Tell me about [Brand]". That question may produce a general company description rather than a recommendation.

Test different customer situations.

For example, a fintech brand could test:

- Which investment platforms are suitable for beginners in India?
- Which platforms offer access to US stocks from India?
- Compare the leading platforms for long-term investors.
- What are the limitations of [Brand]?
- What alternatives are available to [Brand]?

Use the same prompts on both platforms. Record whether the brand appears, where it appears in the answer, what the platform says about it and which sources are cited.

| Metric | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| Brand mention rate | How often the brand appears across tested prompts |
| Recommendation rate | How often it is recommended, not merely mentioned |
| Source citation rate | How often the brand's own website is cited |
| Competitor mention rate | Which alternatives appear more frequently |
| Accuracy | Whether the description of the brand is correct |
| Context | Whether the brand appears in a relevant use case |

For example, if your brand appears in 6 out of 20 prompts, its mention rate is 30%. This is an internal tracking measure, not an official platform score.

## What should a brand do if competitors are mentioned more often?

Start with a comparison of the pages and sources that appear in the answers.

Check whether competitors have clearer product explanations, more useful comparison pages, stronger independent reviews or more current information.

Then identify what your website is missing.

A practical improvement plan could include:

1. Improve the official company and product descriptions.
2. Publish detailed use-case pages for different customer needs.
3. Add transparent pricing and product limitations.
4. Provide original examples and documented evidence.
5. Encourage genuine customer reviews and independent coverage.
6. Correct outdated information across official channels.
7. Check crawler access and technical errors.
8. Repeat the same brand visibility tests every month.

Do not create hundreds of pages simply to include every possible brand-related prompt. A smaller number of useful, well-supported pages is a better starting point.

## Can a brand pay ChatGPT or Perplexity to be recommended?

An organic AI-generated recommendation should not be confused with a paid advertisement.

The presence of a brand in an answer does not prove that the company paid for placement. Similarly, the absence of a brand does not prove that a platform is deliberately excluding it.

Always examine the context in which a brand appears and whether the platform labels any content as advertising or sponsored placement.

## The real objective is accurate brand representation

AI visibility is not just about getting a brand name into an answer. It is about ensuring that the platform can find reliable information and describe the company correctly.

A brand should be able to answer three questions:

- Can people find accurate information about us?
- Can they understand what our products actually do?
- Can independent sources verify important claims about us?

These are useful foundations for both traditional search visibility and AI-driven discovery.

There is no guaranteed method to make ChatGPT or Perplexity recommend a brand. The practical job is to improve the quality, clarity, accessibility and credibility of the information available about it.
