In a groundbreaking move to make AI accessible to everyone, OpenAI has launched a new feature allowing users to interact with ChatGPT via a simple phone call. This innovative step ensures that even those without smartphones or internet access can now experience the capabilities of AI-powered conversations.

#### **What Is 1-800-CHATGPT?**

OpenAI has introduced a toll-free number, **1-800-CHATGPT**, which users can call to engage with ChatGPT. Whether you’re using a landline or a basic flip phone, you can now access the same conversational experience offered by ChatGPT’s Advanced Voice Mode. While the voice feature lacks multimodal interactions (like image and text input), it retains the core conversational AI functionality that ChatGPT is known for.

#### **How Does It Work?**

Calling the 1-800-CHATGPT number connects you to ChatGPT, where you can pose questions, seek advice, or just have a friendly chat. The AI responds in real time, mimicking the conversational experience you’d expect from the app or web platform.

#### **Free Calling for U.S. Users**

To make this feature even more accessible, OpenAI is offering **15 minutes of free calling** for U.S.-based users. This free trial gives users the opportunity to explore the AI’s capabilities without incurring additional costs. However, it’s important to note that **standard carrier fees may apply** if you exceed the free calling limit or are not on a free plan.

#### **Who Benefits from This?**

The introduction of a landline-compatible AI interaction caters to several groups:

1.  **Users Without Smartphones or Internet Access:** Expanding the reach of AI to underserved communities or individuals with limited technology.
2.  **Older Generations:** Offering an easy-to-use solution for those who may not be comfortable with apps or smart devices.
3.  **Professionals on the Go:** Providing quick access to ChatGPT without the need to open an app or sit at a computer.

#### **Why It Matters**

This initiative underscores OpenAI’s mission to make AI universally accessible. By removing barriers like device requirements or internet dependency, OpenAI is democratizing access to conversational AI, ensuring it reaches a broader audience than ever before.

#### **Looking Ahead**

While this is just the beginning, it paves the way for more inclusive AI solutions. As OpenAI continues to innovate, features like 1-800-CHATGPT set the stage for integrating AI into daily life, regardless of technological limitations.
