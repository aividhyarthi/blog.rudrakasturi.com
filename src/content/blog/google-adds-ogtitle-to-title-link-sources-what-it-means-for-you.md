**Google has added the "og:title" tag** to the list of sources it uses to create title links in search results. This change could influence how your page titles are generated by Google and displayed in search results, so let’s break down what it means and how to use it effectively.

#### **What Is "og:title"?**

**og:title** is part of the **Open Graph metadata** used to describe how your content should look when shared on social media platforms like Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn. This tag helps you control how a page title appears when someone shares it.

**Example**: If you have an article about “Top 10 Travel Tips for Summer,” your **og:title** might be something more engaging for social media like, “Top 10 Essential Travel Hacks You Need for a Great Summer!” to catch people’s attention more effectively.

#### **What’s the Update About?**

Google has now added **og:title** to its list of sources for generating **title links** in search results. Here’s what that means:

*   Google can use the content of the **og:title** tag to generate the title link that appears in search results, depending on how relevant and engaging it is compared to other title sources.
*   This can help improve your click-through rate (CTR) by making the title more compelling and matching what users are looking for.

#### **Why This Change Is Important**

*   **More Control Over Your Title Links**: The **og:title** tag can now be used not only for social media but also to influence how your page title appears in Google Search.
*   **Catchier Titles in Search Results**: If your **og:title** is more engaging or click-worthy than your standard page title, Google might choose to use it. This can make your page stand out more in search results, potentially attracting more clicks.

**Example**: Imagine you run a recipe blog, and your article is titled “Best Recipes for a Healthy Breakfast.” You use an **og:title** like “5 Delicious Breakfast Recipes to Start Your Day Right!” Google might choose this **og**for search results because it’s more appealing and gives more of a reason to click.

#### **How to Make the Most of "og"**

*   **Create Engaging Titles**: Use the **og:title** tag to craft **catchy, clear, and relevant titles** that resonate with your audience. This will help Google pick it up as the title in search results, making your link more attractive.
*   **Consistency Matters**: Ensure your **og:title** is still **relevant** to the content of your page. A catchy title is great, but it should still accurately represent what users will find on the page to avoid misleading them.

**Example**: If you have a blog post about managing finances, instead of just “Tips for Managing Finances,” use an **og:title** like “Smart Finance Hacks: How to Budget and Save More Easily!” This makes it more engaging, not just for social media but also potentially for Google’s search results.

### **Why You Should Care?**

This update gives you an opportunity to improve your **title links** in Google Search. The **og:title** tag is now another tool you can use to make your content shine in search results, providing a great opportunity to drive more traffic.

To make the most of this update, review your existing **og:title** tags and craft them to be both **relevant and engaging**. Doing this will help your content stand out more effectively on both social media and in Google Search, giving you better control over how users see and engage with your page.
