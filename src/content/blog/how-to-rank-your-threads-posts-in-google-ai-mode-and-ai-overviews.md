**Quick answer:** Public Threads posts can already end up in Google's index, and reports from 2026 say they are turning up in Google Discover more often too. That is the visibility layer most people miss. Whether a specific post then gets cited inside an AI Overview or AI Mode answer depends on the same basics as any other social post: is the claim clear enough to lift out on its own.

**Best for:** brands and creators active on Threads who want that activity to also pay off in Google and AI search, not only inside the app.

## What is reasonably well reported

- **Threads posts have been indexed by Google since close to launch**, without Meta making a formal announcement about it, according to multiple outlets covering the platform.
- **A "ghost posts" issue surfaced in 2025**, where 24-hour disappearing posts were found searchable on Google after they had expired inside the app; Meta told reporters this was fixed. This is a reminder that "disappearing" inside the app does not always mean invisible to a crawler that caught it in time.
- **Google Discover has shown more Threads content through 2026**, separate from classic search and from AI Overviews specifically.

We did not find an official Google or Meta page describing how a Threads post gets pulled into an AI Overview or AI Mode answer specifically. Everything below is a sensible application of general AI-search practice to this platform, not a confirmed Threads ranking rule.

## What to do

1. **Keep the post public.** Only public posts have ever been reported as reaching Google at all.
2. **Put the answer in the first line.** A Threads post that opens with the actual fact, number or step reads like a snippet an AI system can lift, the same way a strong first sentence works for a web page.
3. **Don't build your main message around a disappearing post.** If something matters enough to want indexed, post it as a normal, persistent post, not a 24-hour one, and don't assume a disappearing post is private just because the app stops showing it.
4. **Cross-post the fuller version to a page you own.** A Threads post is a good distribution unit, not a replacement for a page with your actual content, where you control structure, links and freshness.
5. **Check it yourself.** Search your exact phrase in Google, in Google Discover (if you can check a test device's feed) and in AI Mode. Use the same [weekly Search Console routine](/how-to-use-search-console-for-tiktok-instagram-x-and-youtube-content-a-weekly-routine/) used for Instagram, X and YouTube content.
6. **Keep a short log.** AI Overview behavior for social content is changing through 2026; what triggers a citation this month is not guaranteed to hold next month.

## How this differs from growing Threads followers

Ranking in Google is a different goal from growing your Threads following. A post written purely to be cited by Google (short, answer-first, link-free) is not the same post that performs best for replies and reach inside the app. If follower growth on Threads itself is the goal, see [how to grow your Threads followers](/how-to-grow-your-threads-followers-a-practical-playbook/) instead, and treat AI-search visibility as a secondary benefit of some of your posts, not every post.
