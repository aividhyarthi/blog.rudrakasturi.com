**November 20, 2024** — Google has made updates to its iOS app by adding a new **Page Annotations feature** and providing users with an option to opt out if they choose.

#### **What is the Page Annotations Feature?**

The Page Annotations feature, recently launched on the Google app for iOS, is designed to enhance user interaction with web pages. This feature likely uses AI to provide highlights, summaries, or key insights from web pages, making it easier for users to navigate and understand content. Such tools are part of Google's broader initiative to integrate AI-driven functionality into its suite of apps.

#### **Opting Out of Page Annotations**

Recognizing that not all users may prefer this feature, Google has introduced a clear option to opt out. This ensures users retain control over their browsing and data usage preferences. Opt-out details can be accessed within the app's settings, under the Page Annotations section.

#### **Why Was This Update Made?**

The addition of an opt-out option reflects Google's commitment to transparency and user choice. While Page Annotations provide convenience, some users may have privacy concerns or prefer an unaltered browsing experience. This update allows users to customize their experience in a way that aligns with their preferences.

#### **How to Opt Out**

1.  Open the **Google App** on your iOS device.
2.  Navigate to **Settings**.
3.  Look for the **Page Annotations** feature under the relevant menu.
4.  Toggle the feature off to disable it.

By making this feature optional, Google continues to adapt its services to the diverse needs of its users. This approach strikes a balance between leveraging AI to enhance usability and respecting individual preferences.
