This Podcast features insights from members of the **Google Search team**, including individuals like **John Mueller, Martin Splitt, and Gary Illyes**

**Demystifying SEO for Developers**: Addressing and clarifying common misconceptions that developers often have about SEO.

**SEO Guidance for Small Businesses**: Providing assistance for developers or small business owners who may be struggling with Search Engine Optimization.

**Bridging the Gap between SEOs and Developers**: Offering a candid discussion on what SEO professionals should understand about developers.

**Understanding Googlebot's Mechanics**: Providing a deep dive into **how Googlebot crawls the web**.

**Fundamental Internet Protocols**: Exploring essential internet concepts like **HTTP and TCP** as part of "Debugging the Internet".
