The July 2021 core update is now "essentially complete," according to Google. The Tech Giant finished rolling out the update which was released on July 1, 2021. The full rollout of such an upgrade takes one to two weeks. 

“The July 2021 core upgrade rollout is now effectively complete,” Google reached out to Twitter to let the world know. Google added, "These normally take 1-2 weeks to complete." 

Google had earlier planned this update, but was unable to do so due to a lack of time. Hence, two core updates were released one month after the other. After Google's core update in June 2021, the search engine stated that "some of our planned changes for the June 2021 update aren't quite ready," and that "we're moving ahead with the parts that are, then we'll follow with the remainder with the July 2021 update." This particular change was expected in July, and it has arrived. 

Update to the core in July 2021. On July 1, 2021, Google began rolling out the July 2021 core update. The sibling upgrade, the June 2021 core update, took only two ten-days to fully roll out, whereas this update took twelve days. Essentially the facts advocate that on July 1, 2021, Google began pushing out the July 2021 core update at noon. At roughly 1 p.m. ET today, July 12th, this update was completed. As previously announced, the June 2021 core update began rolling out on Wednesday, June 2nd. This, like other core updates, was a global update that affected all regions, languages, and web site categories. 

Presumably, this is a standard Google "wide core upgrade" that comes out every few months or so.
