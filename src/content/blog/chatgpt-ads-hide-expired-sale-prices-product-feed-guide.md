An expired discount can create a bad customer experience. A shopper sees a promotional price in an advertisement, clicks through and discovers that the offer has ended. The retailer may lose trust, receive complaints or create unnecessary support work.

OpenAI has added a setting for product-feed ad groups that helps advertisers hide sale prices after a promotion ends. The setting is called **Hide product sale prices** and appears under **Product display** when editing the ad group.

## What does the setting do?

It allows the advertiser to hide sale prices in the product-feed ad display so the regular price is shown again. The product feed can continue to contain both regular and sale price values.

This is useful when the promotion has ended but the feed still includes a sale price field, or when a retailer wants to stop displaying the discount without rebuilding the product feed.

## Step-by-step: hide expired sale prices

1. Sign in to ChatGPT Ads Manager.
2. Open the campaign that contains the relevant product-feed ad group.
3. Select the product-feed ad group you want to update.
4. Choose **Edit**.
5. Find the **Product display** section.
6. Select **Hide product sale prices**.
7. Save or publish the ad group changes using the controls shown in your account.
8. Preview the product ad and confirm that the regular price is displayed.
9. Check the destination product page to ensure that the price and availability match.

The exact button labels around saving may vary as the interface changes. The named setting and its location under Product display are the details reported in the November 25 update.

## Should you remove the sale price from the feed too?

Not necessarily. The new control is designed to hide sale prices in the ad display. The product update says advertisers can keep both prices in the feed.

Do not delete or overwrite feed data without understanding how your product catalogue uses it. The feed may be shared with other campaigns or commerce channels.

## A retail promotion checklist

Before a sale begins:

- Confirm the sale price, start date and end date in the product catalogue.
- Check that the regular price remains accurate.
- Ensure the landing page reflects the advertised offer.
- Verify product variants, stock and shipping details.
- Record which ad groups use the promotion.

When the sale ends:

- Enable Hide product sale prices where appropriate.
- Confirm the regular price is shown in the ad preview.
- Check the live product page and product feed.
- Review any scheduled campaigns that reuse the same products.
- Keep a record of the change and the person who approved it.

## Common mistakes to avoid

**Hiding the sale price but leaving the landing page discounted:** The ad and page may no longer communicate the same offer. Check both.

**Assuming the setting changes every campaign:** The update describes a product-feed ad group setting. Verify each relevant ad group.

**Deleting catalogue sale-price data unnecessarily:** The display setting may be sufficient. Check the feed workflow before changing source data.

**Forgetting product variants:** A sale may end on one size or colour but remain active on another. Validate the actual product variants.

## Why this small control matters

Retail advertising depends on consistency. The product image, title, price, availability and landing page should agree. A stale promotional price can damage trust even if the rest of the advertisement is correct.

The new control gives advertisers a simpler way to manage the display of sale prices in ChatGPT product-feed ads. It does not replace the retailer's responsibility to keep catalogue and landing-page information accurate.

**Treat the product feed as a live business record, not just an advertising file.**
