## Query Fan-Out: One Question, Many Searches

**Mara:** The core claim here is that AI search engines — Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini — do not process your question as typed. They decompose it, expand it, and run multiple versions simultaneously before you see a single word of the answer.

**Pip:** The post puts it plainly: "Instead of ten blue links, the user receives a synthesised explanation with supporting citations — all drawn from the multi-directional search that happened behind the scenes. From a brand's perspective, this is a citation opportunity that either you earned or your competitor did."

**Mara:** That last clause is the real stakes. Citation and ranking are now two separate outcomes. A competitor sitting at position three on Google could still appear more often in AI Overviews if their content covers a topic more completely.

**Pip:** And the mechanism behind that is documented — not just observed. Google Patent US11663201B2, filed 2018, granted May 2023, formally names eight sub-query types the system generates from a single input: equivalent queries, follow-ups, generalisations, specifications, canonicalisations, language translations, entailments, and clarifications.

**Mara:** All of them invisible to the user. The post walks through a worked example — "best cleanser for teenage girls with oily skin" — and shows how the AI simultaneously generates a Spanish-language version, an implied follow-on about salicylic acid, and a broader query about oily skin generally. Eight searches, one prompt, zero visibility to the person who typed it.

**Pip:** Every major platform does this under a different name. Google calls it multi-query retrieval. OpenAI calls it query rewriting. Copilot calls it query expansion. Grok runs a constrained version focused on authoritative sources. Different branding, same underlying behavior.

**Mara:** The practical reframe the post offers is this: stop asking "did I target the right keyword?" and start asking "does this page comprehensively answer the full set of questions someone would logically ask about this topic?" Topic authority, not keyword match, is what earns citations across the fan-out.

**Pip:** So thin single-keyword pages keep their rankings and quietly disappear from AI answers at the same time.

**Mara:** Exactly — and that divergence is the reason both metrics now need to be tracked separately.
