This is not a blog about AI content.  
This is not a blog about a YouTube channel.  
This is not even a blog about money.

This is a blog about **how media loses control of value creation in the age of AI driven buzz**.

Let me explain using a recent real example that many of you have seen.

## The moment everything started

A global tool published a report saying an Indian AI YouTube channel _may_ be earning millions of dollars a year if monetised.

That one sentence did something powerful.

Media picked it up.  
Creators debated it.  
Reddit threads exploded.  
Google search interest spiked overnight.

If you look at Google Trends, the brand name went from flat to breakout in a few days.

No ads.  
No paid PR.  
No media buying.

Just one estimate.

## What actually happened here

From an ORM and branding point of view, this was textbook.

A third party mentioned the brand.  
A big number entered the ecosystem.  
Media amplified it.  
Creators validated it by debating it.

The brand got:  
Attention  
Authority  
Search demand  
Industry recognition

This is what people pay crores for.

![](https://blog.rudrakasturi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Screenshot-2026-01-06-at-8.54.15-AM.webp)

**Typically breaking news gets such high buzz in google trends.**

## Let us do very simple math

If this kind of buzz was created intentionally through paid PR, what would it cost?

Assume conservative numbers.

10 English publishers  
₹5 lakh per article

5 regional publishers  
₹2 lakh per article

Add coordination and amplification

You are easily looking at **₹1 crore plus**.

That is the real market cost of this level of attention.

But here, that money was never spent.

The brand got the buzz for free.

**Trusted media reports created the perception that this was the best monetised AI channel in India.**

![Trusted media reports created the perception that this was the best monetised AI channel in India.](https://blog.rudrakasturi.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Screenshot-2026-01-06-at-9.02.22-AM.webp)

## Now comes the uncomfortable question

Who paid for it then?

The answer is simple.

The media did.

## What did publishers actually earn from this story?

Let us be honest.

Most such articles would get:  
40,000 to 60,000 pageviews

In India, for tech and media traffic, that translates to:  
Very low RPM  
Low advertiser interest  
Minimal revenue

In simple terms:  
The revenue from one such article would not even cover a Starbucks bill.  
In many cases, not even a roadside chai.

This is not exaggeration.  
This is Indian digital media economics.

## So why does media still do it?

Because media is not wrong. Media is rushed.

In the AI era:  
Speed matters  
Numbers attract clicks  
Estimates look authoritative

A tool said “may earn”.  
Headlines converted it to “earns”.

Not out of malice.  
Out of pressure.

But that one shortcut changes everything.

## The real problem is not accuracy. It is value leakage.

The media did not lie.  
But it did not **frame** the story either.

What was missing:  
Clear explanation of estimates  
Clear mention of monetisation limits  
Clear India RPM context  
Clear distinction between branding and revenue

Without this, the story shifts value.

The brand gains authority.  
The audience gains confusion.  
The publisher gains traffic, not trust.

This is value leakage.

## Reddit exposed the truth faster than newsrooms

What is interesting is that Reddit creators immediately said:

“SocialBlade shows high earnings, but I earn nothing.”  
“These tools are misleading.”  
“Shorts money in India is terrible.”

Creators know the reality.  
Media often does not pause to explain it.

That gap is dangerous.

## This is not about blaming creators or tools

Creators did not fake anything.  
Tools did what tools do.  
They estimate.

The issue is how media treats estimates as outcomes.

In the AI era:  
Estimates spread faster than facts.  
Perception travels faster than revenue.

That is ORM, whether intentional or not.

## Why this matters more in 2026 than ever before

Today:  
ORM builds brands faster than advertising.  
Search spikes are currency.  
Perception creates leverage.

Every time media amplifies a speculative number:  
It builds someone’s brand equity.  
It does unpaid marketing.  
It gives away authority.

And it gets almost nothing in return.

## My thoughts

This episode is not about AI slop.  
It is not about YouTube money.  
It is not about one channel.

It is a lesson in how:  
Attention is created  
Brands are built  
Value quietly shifts hands

In this case, the brand gained crores worth of visibility.  
The media earned coffee money.

Not because media is weak.  
But because media underestimated its own power.

In the age of AI, **ORM is money**.  
And media should think twice before giving it away for free.

That is all.

**My two cents.**
