## What’s New?

Google has officially confirmed: **Clicks from AI Overviews and AI Mode now count towards your total traffic in Search Console.**

So if someone discovers your website via those fancy new AI answers at the top of Google Search (a.k.a. AI Overviews), that traffic now **shows up inside your “Web” report** in Search Console.

**Earlier?**  
These AI features were _mysterious zones_ - no traffic, no reporting, just vibes.

**Now?**  
AI Mode traffic joins the same pool as regular Google search clicks. You’ll see it in your normal **Performance Report** under “Search Type: Web.”

## Why Does This Matter?

For months, SEOs have been asking:

> **_“Where’s my traffic going if Google is answering everything with AI?”_**

Well, now you can start measuring it.

### What counts now:

*   AI Overviews (a.k.a. AI snapshots in search)
*   AI Mode (when users click "Generate" or engage with longer AI answers)
*   Featured answers that use your content

All of the above now get counted toward your **total Search Console traffic.**

## Where Will I See It?

In Search Console:

*   Go to **Performance > Search results**
*   Filter by **Search type: Web**
*   Look for **new clicks/impressions** that weren’t there before these may come from AI answers

Remember: there’s **no separate filter for “AI”** (yet). It all gets added to your normal Web traffic.

## How Can I Tell What Came from AI?

You technically can’t isolate just AI Overview clicks at least for now. But here’s what you can do:

### 1\. **Watch for unexplained traffic spikes**

If your keyword rankings haven’t changed but clicks increased, **AI Mode** may be giving you a lift.

### 2\. **Use Google Analytics**

You can track:

*   Time on page
*   Conversions
*   Bounce rate

According to Google, clicks from AI Overviews are **"higher quality"** meaning, users spend more time and engage more.

## What Should You Do Now?

**Keep optimizing for helpful, authoritative answers**  
AI Overviews pull from trusted, relevant content. So content quality still wins.

**Keep measuring**  
Combine Search Console + Google Analytics to get the full picture:

*   Are AI clicks leading to engagement?
*   Are AI answers hurting or helping your CTR?
