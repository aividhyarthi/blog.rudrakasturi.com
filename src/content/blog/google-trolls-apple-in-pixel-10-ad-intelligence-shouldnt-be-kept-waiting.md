_Google’s latest Pixel 10 ad lands a direct jab at Apple, mocking Siri’s delayed AI upgrade and positioning Pixel as the “ready now” smart choice. Is it just marketing, or is the AI gap real? Watch the video and decide._

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZR\_6Z1IDD8s&t=30s

In its latest Pixel 10 ad, Google doesn’t just launch a new phone - it fires shots straight at Apple’s slow AI game.

The ad, making waves online, pokes fun at Apple’s much-hyped “Apple Intelligence” and the years-long delay in bringing real AI features to iPhones. Google’s message is clear: Pixel AI is ready now, not “coming soon.”

While Apple fans wait for Siri to finally graduate, Google is betting Indian and global users want smart, instant answers today not in the next software update.

Watch the ad, decide who’s winning the AI race and ask yourself, is your phone actually smart, or just waiting for an upgrade?
