OpenAI has added more audience-focused reporting fields to ChatGPT Ads, including Unique CTR, Unique CPC and Cost per 1,000 Reached.

The names suggest a move from measuring activity against total impressions towards measuring engagement and cost against the number of people reached. That is useful because ten clicks from ten different people are not the same as ten clicks generated by one person returning repeatedly.

There is an important documentation gap. OpenAI's November 25 update announced these fields, but the public reporting documentation reviewed for this guide does not yet provide a complete calculation specification for every unique metric. The explanations below distinguish standard advertising definitions from working interpretations that advertisers should validate against the live Ads Manager field descriptions or API.

## 1. What is Unique CTR?

CTR normally measures clicks divided by impressions.

**Standard CTR = Clicks ÷ Impressions × 100**

Unique CTR is intended to measure engagement in relation to unique people rather than total exposures. A likely working interpretation is:

**Indicative Unique CTR = Unique clickers ÷ Reach × 100**

Example:

- Estimated Reach: 20,000
- People who clicked: 600
- Indicative Unique CTR: 600 ÷ 20,000 × 100 = 3%

This example explains the concept. It is not a confirmed OpenAI formula. Confirm whether the Ads Manager or API field uses unique clickers, Click Reach or another denominator before publishing the metric externally.

## 2. What is Unique CPC?

Standard CPC is total spend divided by clicks.

**Standard CPC = Spend ÷ Clicks**

Unique CPC is intended to express cost against unique click activity rather than every click event. A likely working interpretation is:

**Indicative Unique CPC = Spend ÷ Unique clickers**

Example:

- Spend: $600
- Unique clickers: 300
- Indicative Unique CPC: $2

Again, this is an explanatory interpretation, not a published OpenAI calculation rule. Do not assume it is identical to standard CPC.

## 3. What is Cost per 1,000 Reached?

This metric expresses the amount spent to reach 1,000 estimated unique people.

**Cost per 1,000 Reached = Spend ÷ Reach × 1,000**

Example:

- Spend: $900
- Reach: 30,000
- Cost per 1,000 Reached: $900 ÷ 30,000 × 1,000 = $30

This is different from CPM, which is generally calculated against 1,000 impressions.

## 4. CPM versus cost per 1,000 reached

| Metric | Denominator | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| CPM | Impressions | Cost of 1,000 ad exposures |
| Cost per 1,000 Reached | Estimated unique Reach | Cost of reaching 1,000 people |
| CPC | Clicks | Average cost per click event |
| Unique CPC | Unique click activity, subject to platform definition | Cost in relation to distinct clickers |
| CTR | Impressions | Clicks relative to exposures |
| Unique CTR | Unique audience, subject to platform definition | Engagement relative to distinct people |

## 5. A worked campaign example

Imagine a ChatGPT Ads campaign with:

- Spend: $1,200
- Impressions: 100,000
- Estimated Reach: 40,000
- Clicks: 2,000
- Unique clickers: 1,000

The standard metrics are:

- CPM = $1,200 ÷ 100,000 × 1,000 = $12
- CPC = $1,200 ÷ 2,000 = $0.60
- CTR = 2,000 ÷ 100,000 × 100 = 2%
- Frequency = 100,000 ÷ 40,000 = 2.5

Using the indicative unique formulas:

- Cost per 1,000 Reached = $1,200 ÷ 40,000 × 1,000 = $30
- Indicative Unique CTR = 1,000 ÷ 40,000 × 100 = 2.5%
- Indicative Unique CPC = $1,200 ÷ 1,000 = $1.20

Notice that unique CPC can be higher than standard CPC because the number of unique clickers is lower than the total number of click events.

## 6. How to use these metrics together

- Use Reach to understand audience scale.
- Use Frequency to understand repeated exposure.
- Use Cost per 1,000 Reached to compare the cost of audience coverage.
- Use Unique CTR to assess whether distinct people are responding.
- Use Unique CPC to understand the cost of generating unique click activity.
- Use conversions and customer value to decide whether the campaign is commercially successful.

Do not optimise for the cheapest cost per reached thousand if the campaign objective is sales. Cheap reach can still be low-quality reach.

## 7. What advertisers must verify before reporting

1. Confirm the exact field definition in the current Ads Manager or API.
2. Confirm whether Unique CTR uses unique clicks, Click Reach or another field.
3. Confirm whether Unique CPC uses unique clickers or a different click metric.
4. Check attribution windows and reporting time zones.
5. Compare identical date ranges and campaign scopes.
6. Do not compare platform Reach figures as if all platforms use identical identity methods.

## The takeaway

These metrics can help advertisers understand how efficiently ChatGPT Ads reaches and engages people. But the metric name alone is not a calculation specification.

Until OpenAI publishes the full formula and estimation methodology for every unique field, use the live field descriptions as the source of truth and label any independently calculated figures clearly.

**Better measurement begins with knowing exactly what is being counted.**
