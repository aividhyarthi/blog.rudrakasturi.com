Search engines aren’t what they used to be.  
Neither is SEO.  
Today, AI runs the show — from what results appear, to how customers discover brands, to how trust is built online.

If your growth strategy still treats SEO like a "keyword game," you’re already playing last season’s match.  
The real question today is: **How do you find an SEO expert who can help you win in the AI-driven future?**

Here’s what serious founders need to know.

## Understand: SEO Is Now About Training the Algorithms

Forget stuffing keywords into landing pages.  
Modern SEO is about **training AI models** (like Google's Search Generative Experience) to _understand, trust, and promote_ your brand.

*   Building authority across an entire topic
*   Structuring your content so AI can crawl and _connect the dots_
*   Creating genuine, layered, trustworthy experiences — not just blogs

**Example:**  
In 2018, you could launch a new product, run 20 guest posts, and climb rankings.  
In 2025? Google's AI will check:

*   Optimize for _topics_ and _search intent_, not just keywords
*   Build _knowledge graphs_ around your brand
*   Understand how **Google's AI** evaluates **EEAT** (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trustworthiness)
*   Use **AI tools** to generate, cluster, and plan content at scale

If your SEO expert doesn't think like this, you're running uphill.

**Mindset Check**: Your SEO hire must be someone who thinks like an AI _trainer_, not just a "traffic hacker."

## What to Look For in an AI-First SEO Hire

Here’s what separates serious players from old-school "keyword people":

### a. Strategic Brains

They think 12-18 months ahead, not just about "ranking tomorrow."  
They can map out how **topical authority**, **content velocity**, and **AI search alignment** drive real market positioning.

### b. Technical Muscle

They understand how to structure content for AI:

*   Schema markup
*   Entity SEO
*   Internal link hubs
*   Programmatic SEO for scale

They can speak Googlebot’s language _and_ customer language.

### c. AI Tool Fluency

They know how to leverage tools like SurferSEO, Frase, NeuronWriter, ChatGPT-4o — without letting automation produce trash.  
They use AI to _amplify strategy_, not to mindlessly flood content.

### d. Content Judgment

They can brief writers, designers, and AI models on _what great content_ looks like —  
content that _earns attention_ and _builds brand memory_.

## How to Interview an AI-SEO Strategist Properly

**Scenario-Based Testing**  
Ask: "We’re launching a new AI course. Outline your 6-month SEO plan to dominate the category."

**Reverse Q&A**  
See what questions _they_ ask you.  
Smart SEOs dig into your product, audience, goals — not just "how many blogs you want."

**Live Tool Walkthroughs**  
Ask them to show you, live, how they use an AI SEO tool to build a content cluster.

**Portfolio Proof, Not Promises**  
Ask: "Show me an example where you helped a brand earn _topic authority_ in a space — not just traffic bumps."

If they can’t show strategic wins (beyond ranking a few blog posts), move on.

## Red Flags to Watch Like a Hawk

They still talk about "keyword density" like it’s 2010  
They have no clue about Google's SGE (Search Generative Experience)  
They’re afraid of AI tools or claim "pure human content is always better" without nuance  
They overpromise ("Rank #1 in 30 days!") — serious SEO takes compounding, not hacks

## Bonus Strategy: Go Fractional if Needed

If you're not ready for a full-time SEO strategist, **hire a fractional SEO lead** who deeply understands AI search.

The right fractional expert will:

*   Build your content and authority roadmap
*   Guide your team (or freelancers)
*   Monitor AI search updates monthly

At a fraction of the cost — and with 10x the experience of average in-house hires.

## The Hiring Playbook

Here’s exactly how a smart CEO should structure the hiring process:

Step

What You Do

1\. Audit First

Ask candidates to audit your website and propose 2-3 AI-SEO strategies.

2\. Scenario Testing

Give them a hypothetical: "You're launching a new AI product. How would you dominate search?"

3\. Tool Familiarity

Ask: Which AI SEO tools do you use, and how? Get a live demo if possible.

4\. Portfolio Proof

Demand real examples: "Show me a 3-month SEO turnaround you achieved."

5\. Culture Check

Make sure they love **learning fast**, because AI search is evolving _daily_.

# My Take:

**The future of search belongs to brands who understand how AI discovers, evaluates, and rewards expertise.**

The right AI-SEO hire isn’t just optimizing pages.  
They’re optimizing your company’s destiny in a machine-driven world.

Hire accordingly.
