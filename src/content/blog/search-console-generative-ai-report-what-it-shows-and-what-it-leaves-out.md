Search Console now includes a generative AI performance report. Industry coverage says it started with a limited group of site owners in the UK in June 2026 and reached all countries by the end of August. Sources differ slightly on the dates, so check your own Search Console to see whether you have it.

## What the report shows

According to Google's help page and industry coverage:

- **Impressions** when your URL appears in AI Overviews and AI Mode in Search, and in generative AI features in Discover.
- The **pages** that appear, and how impressions change over time.
- A breakdown by dimensions such as **country and device**.

An impression is counted when a link to your page appears inside one of these features, for example as a citation.

## What it leaves out

- **No clicks, click-through rate or position.** The report does not tell you how many people clicked.
- **No queries.** You can see which pages appear, not the searches that triggered them.
- **AI Overviews and AI Mode are reported together**, according to at least one write-up, so you cannot split them.
- **Clicks from AI links appear in the normal Web search report**, mixed in with other clicks. There is no filter to separate them.
- Properties with very few AI impressions may not see the report at all.

## How to use it

1. **Find your most visible pages.** These are the pages Google already treats as useful sources. Keep them accurate and up to date.
2. **Compare with the Web search report.** If a page has many AI impressions but clicks are flat, the answer on Google may already be enough for most readers. Consider adding detail that a short answer cannot cover.
3. **Check your controls.** Search Console also has a setting to opt out of these features. If you are opted out, you will not appear. See [our guide to the AI controls](/how-to-see-and-control-ai-overviews-and-ai-mode-in-search-console/).
4. **Track over weeks, not days.** Counts move with product tests and rollouts.

Because the report has no click data, treat it as a measure of visibility only, and keep tracking clicks and conversions separately.
