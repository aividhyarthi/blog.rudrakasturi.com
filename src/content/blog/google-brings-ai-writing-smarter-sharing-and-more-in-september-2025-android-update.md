Google has announced its latest batch of Android features, rolling out to devices globally this September. The update introduces AI-powered writing tools, improvements to media sharing, new ways to use emoji, and even a playful selfie-to-Android bot trick.

## Revise Your Writing with AI in Gboard

The biggest upgrade comes to **Gboard**, Google’s keyboard app. Users can now access **AI-powered writing assistance** directly while typing:

*   Adjust the tone of text: formal, expressive, or concise.
*   Fix grammar and spelling automatically.
*   Get rewrite suggestions, helping users refine emails, messages, and posts without leaving the keyboard.

The feature is designed to keep writing support accessible and contextual, without needing to switch apps.

## Remix Emoji with Emoji Kitchen

Google’s playful **Emoji Kitchen** is getting a refresh. The new update makes it easier to:

*   Browse remixed emoji stickers.
*   Favorite and save frequently used stickers.
*   Explore creative combinations to add more fun and personality in chats.

## Share Audio with a Friend or a Group

A new audio-sharing feature takes advantage of **LE Audio Bluetooth technology**. With it, users can:

*   Pair two headphones to listen to the same audio source.
*   Create private audio broadcasts that others can join via QR codes.

This means music, podcasts, or videos can be enjoyed together without sharing earbuds.

## Quicker and Smarter Media Sharing

**Quick Share**, Google’s cross-device file transfer system, is now more intuitive:

*   Redesigned interface with previews before sending.
*   Progress indicator to track transfers.
*   One-tap toggling between “send” and “receive” modes.

This makes sending photos and videos simpler, especially in group settings or when sharing multiple files.

## Bonus: Androidify Returns

For fun, Google has brought back **Androidify** in a new form. By taking a selfie, users can instantly generate a personalized Android bot version of themselves - a nostalgic callback to the early Android days.

## Why It Matters

This feature drop reflects Google’s dual focus:

*   **AI at the core**: by embedding writing assistance directly in Gboard, Google makes AI productivity tools everyday and accessible.
*   **Connected experiences**: from shared audio to easier file transfers, the update pushes Android towards smoother, more social interactions.
*   **Playful identity**: with Androidify, Google reminds users that Android is not only functional, but fun.

## Availability

The new features will roll out gradually starting September 2025 across Android devices, with some requiring the latest system updates and Google Play Services.
