Google’s latest report on **Gen Z trends and search behaviour in India** has a clear message for brands, publishers, and marketers: if you want to connect with Gen Z when they are most ready to act, you need to meet them in search.

### The Numbers That Stand Out

*   **56% Lift in Conversions**: Globally, advertisers running Google Search paid campaigns see more than half again as many conversions compared to before.
*   **Daily Search Habit**: 79% of Gen Z in India use Google and/or YouTube daily often to explore, compare, and make decisions.
*   **YouTube Preference**: If they had to choose only one platform, YouTube comes first for Gen Z in India, above Instagram and television.

### Why This Matters

Search is where intent meets action. When someone searches “best AI workflow automation tool” or “nearest bookstore open now,” they are in decision mode. For brands, this is the highest-value moment to appear.

For **journalists**, this means:

*   When big stories break, appearing at the top of search ensures your reporting is seen by those actively seeking updates.

For **SaaS & B2B companies**:

*   Search Ads can place you directly in front of potential customers looking for solutions in real time.

For **e-commerce**:

*   High-intent searches like “buy running shoes online” or “best noise-cancelling headphones” are your fastest path to conversions.

### How to Act on This Insight

1.  **Blend Paid and Organic Search**  
    Relying solely on SEO can mean missing out on high-intent clicks. Use Search Ads to secure prime real estate while organic rankings build.
2.  **Match Ad Copy to Intent**  
    Write ads that directly address what the searcher wants answer the query in the headline and offer a clear next step.
3.  **Leverage Google’s AI Tools**  
    Campaign types like **Performance Max** and **Local Inventory Ads** help bridge online intent with offline sales. Reliance Digital’s campaign during Durga Puja drove **129% more store visits** and a **35% sales increase** using this approach.
4.  **Track and Optimize**  
    Monitor conversion rates, cost per acquisition, and the search terms triggering your ads. Use this data to refine targeting and improve ROI.

### The Takeway Point For You:

Gen Z isn’t just scrolling, they are searching with purpose. Appearing at that exact moment of intent is one of the most powerful ways to drive action, and Search Ads remain one of the most effective tools to make that happen.

Whether you are a newsroom, a SaaS founder, or a retail brand, the question is not if Gen Z is searching, it’s whether you are there when they do.
