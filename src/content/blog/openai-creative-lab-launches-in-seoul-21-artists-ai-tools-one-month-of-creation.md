**13th August 2025 – Seoul, South Korea**

OpenAI has officially launched the **OpenAI Creative Lab**, a global artist program aimed at empowering visual storytellers, designers, and creators to explore new forms of creativity using AI.

The first edition of this program is taking place in **Seoul**, bringing together **21 selected artists** for a month-long creative residency. These artists will use OpenAI’s tools like DALL·E and ChatGPT to build new art projects, experiment with AI-assisted storytelling, and collaborate with fellow creators.

### What is OpenAI Creative Lab?

OpenAI Creative Lab is a new initiative designed to:

*   **Democratize access** to advanced creative tools
*   **Support artists** at every stage of their journey
*   **Create safe spaces** for experimentation, learning, and sharing ideas

OpenAI says the program is built on a simple idea: **when artists lead and tools follow, new forms emerge.**

Over the coming weeks, OpenAI will introduce each of its inaugural Fellows and share updates from the Seoul lab through its social media channels.

### Why It Matters

This is the first time OpenAI has launched a structured program focused exclusively on artists. It signals a shift from just building AI tools to **actively enabling creativity** through global community programs.

More Creative Labs are expected to be launched in different cities around the world in the coming months.
