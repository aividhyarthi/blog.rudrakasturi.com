Our new Google DeepMind image generation and editing model nicknamed **Nano Banana** is now part of the Gemini app, opening up a new world of creativity. Whether you want to see your pet as a comic-book hero or imagine a surreal scene from your childhood, this update makes it easier than ever.

With Nano Banana, you can:

*   Keep a person or pet consistent across multiple edits
*   Blend different photos into a single frame
*   Alter one part of an image while leaving the rest untouched
*   Transfer the style of one picture onto another object
*   Preserve important details while experimenting with new looks

Here are 10 examples of what’s possible. Each one shows how the model balances realism with imagination and gives you control to shape the picture exactly as you want.
