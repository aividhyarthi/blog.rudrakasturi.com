Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode will become more personally relevant by using a person's recent search context. The detail appeared in Google's November 23 announcement about Personal Intelligence. It describes a change to the answer experience, not a confirmed new ranking factor for publishers.

## What Google actually announced

Google says people will start seeing more personally relevant responses in AI Overviews and AI Mode, tailored to recent search context. Its example is a person researching and planning a trip who can continue a later search without repeating the earlier background.

The same post separately announced optional connections for Google Wallet, Drive and the Gemini app in AI Mode. Those integrations are not the same thing as recent search context. Wallet was beginning to roll out, while Drive and Gemini app support were described as following in the coming weeks.

The distinction matters. Google has described two related forms of context: recent search activity, and information from apps a person deliberately connects. The announcement does not say publishers can see a user's private search history or that this private context becomes an organic ranking signal.

## What changes for the person searching

A user may receive an answer that takes account of what they were researching earlier. Two people entering similar words could therefore receive different wording or follow-up suggestions if their recent search journeys differ.

Google has not published a complete description of when the context is used, how much history is considered or how the feature behaves across every market and account. Users can adjust or turn off personalisation in Search settings.

## What this means for publishers and SEO teams

The immediate challenge is measurement. A publisher may test a query once, see an AI Overview and assume the same answer will appear for every searcher. Context-aware responses make that assumption weaker.

Our practical recommendation is to track a fixed set of prompts rather than rely on one screenshot. Record the date, location, device, query, whether an AI Overview appeared, the answer's key points, cited sources and follow-up links. Repeat the same test in a clean session and after a relevant search journey.

## What to do now

1. Build a fixed list of 10 to 20 important queries.
2. Record whether an AI Overview appears and which sources it cites.
3. Repeat tests in a fresh session and after a relevant search journey.
4. Keep query, date, location and device consistent when comparing results.
5. Do not treat one personalised response as evidence of a ranking change.

## Questions about personalisation in AI Overviews

### Does Google AI Overviews use recent search context?

Google says it will show more personally relevant responses in AI Overviews and AI Mode, tailored to recent search context. It has not published every condition that determines when this happens.

### Is this the same as connecting Google Wallet or Drive?

No. App connections are optional integrations that can add context from selected services. Recent search context is a separate part of the announcement.

### Does personalisation change website rankings?

Google's announcement does not establish that a person's private search context is an organic ranking signal. Publishers should measure visibility and referrals rather than assume a ranking change.
