OpenAI says ChatGPT Space began rolling out globally to Plus users aged 18 and older on November 20, 2026. Space is designed as a place to create interactive pages and presentations with ChatGPT, invite other people to collaborate and, in some cases, connect pages to apps and tools so that content can update automatically.

The change is more than a new name for a file area. It points to ChatGPT becoming a workspace where people can build and maintain useful artefacts, rather than only exchange messages.

## What ChatGPT Space includes

According to OpenAI's release notes, users can create interactive pages and presentations and invite other people with Space access to work on them. Pages and slides can also be configured to update using connected apps and tools.

OpenAI gives examples such as a to-do list that updates itself, a study guide with an interactive 3D model and a project plan that reflects decisions from Slack.

The key idea is that a document or presentation can become a living workspace. Instead of generating a file once and leaving it unchanged, users can connect it to information that changes over time.

## Who can use it

![ChatGPT's Business plan page listing "ChatGPT Space to organize projects and files you build with AI" as a feature alongside Dots agents and connected apps](/images/chatgpt-space-plus-rollout/chatgpt-business-plan-space-feature.png)

OpenAI says the rollout is global for Plus users aged 18 and older. Access to a shared page or presentation requires permission and a signed-in account with Space access. OpenAI lists Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise accounts as eligible to open shared content.

Free users cannot open shared Space pages or presentations. OpenAI also says public links are not available.

Space is available on the web, desktop app and iOS and Android for creating and editing pages. The release notes say it replaces Library and becomes the home for pages, slides, images and uploaded files.

## Why this matters for work

A normal chatbot interaction often ends when the answer is delivered. A workspace changes the relationship. A user can create an artefact, share it, ask for revisions and connect it to other information.

For a marketing team, that could mean a campaign plan that reflects changes in a connected project tool. For a student, it could mean a study guide that can be expanded over time. For a small business, it could mean a recurring task list or an operational presentation.

These are possible workflows based on the product capabilities described by OpenAI, not guarantees that every app or data source will be supported in every account.

## What teams should check before using it

1. Confirm which account types in the team have access.
2. Check whether the connected app is supported and authorised.
3. Review what information is being shared with collaborators.
4. Decide who can edit the page and who should only view it.
5. Test whether automatic updates reflect the correct source data.
6. Keep a review process for financial, legal, customer or operational information.

A self-updating page can save time, but it can also spread an incorrect source value if the connected system is wrong.

## What this means for Indian users

The global Plus rollout makes the feature relevant to Indian professionals, educators and small businesses with eligible accounts. However, access to specific connected apps and collaboration features may depend on the services a user has connected and the permissions available in their region.

For organisations, the practical question is whether Space fits existing workflows and information governance rules. It should not become an informal place to store sensitive business information without checking the organisation's policies.

## The larger shift

ChatGPT is moving from a conversation interface towards a space where outputs can be built, shared and maintained. That could make AI more useful for ongoing work because the result is no longer just an answer in a chat window.

The real value will depend on reliability, permissions, connected app coverage and how easily teams can review changes.
