Microsoft is adding URL inclusion controls to AI Max for Search campaigns. The November 19, 2026 update gives advertisers more control over which pages the system can use when selecting landing destinations and generating or matching ad content.

This is a useful development for performance marketers and SEO teams because it makes page-level website structure more important to paid search automation. It is not a change to organic search ranking.

## What are AI Max URL inclusions?

AI Max uses automation to help match searches, customise ad text and select landing pages. Microsoft says URL inclusions let advertisers specify parts of a website that AI Max can use.

The selected pages can serve two purposes:

1. They can be used as destinations for traffic.
2. They can provide information for text customisation and search term matching.

Microsoft's example is a retailer that wants a campaign to use gift guide pages. By including those URLs, the advertiser can guide the system towards those pages and allow it to draw relevant information from them.

## Why does this matter to SEO teams?

A website may contain hundreds or thousands of pages, but not every page is suitable as a paid landing page.

A category page, product page, buying guide and customer support page have different purposes. If automated campaigns can draw on a broad set of URLs, advertisers need to understand which pages are accurate, current and commercially appropriate.

This creates a practical connection between SEO content architecture and paid search operations. SEO teams often know which pages answer specific search intents. That knowledge can help performance teams define the right URL groups.

## How to prepare your website

**Map pages to intent:** Identify which pages answer research, comparison, purchase and support queries.

**Keep content current:** Check prices, product availability, offers, specifications and claims before allowing pages to inform ad copy.

**Avoid thin or duplicate destinations:** Pages with little unique value may create poor landing experiences and confuse campaign measurement.

**Use clear page titles and content:** A page should make its subject and purpose obvious to both users and automated systems.

**Track landing page performance:** Measure conversion rate, bounce or engagement signals, revenue and cost per acquisition by final URL.

## A practical URL inclusion workflow

1. Export the pages currently receiving paid search traffic.
2. Group URLs by product, category, service and informational intent.
3. Identify pages that are outdated, thin, restricted or not intended for acquisition.
4. Select the pages appropriate for each campaign.
5. Add URL inclusion rules in eligible AI Max campaigns.
6. Review search term and landing page reports after the change.
7. Compare conversion quality and cost with the previous setup.

Microsoft's documentation describes URL inclusion conditions and notes that eligibility requirements apply. Advertisers should confirm that their account and campaign qualify before planning a rollout.

## Does this affect organic SEO or GEO?

The URL inclusion feature controls paid campaign behaviour. It does not directly tell Bing or Google to rank a page higher organically, and it does not guarantee that a page will be cited in an AI-generated answer.

However, the underlying lesson is relevant to SEO, GEO and AEO: pages should have clear purpose, accurate information and a structure that makes their subject easy to understand.

For organic visibility, continue to focus on crawlability, indexation, helpful content, internal linking, clear entities and reliable evidence. For paid search, use the new controls to guide automated campaigns towards the right destinations.

## Frequently asked questions

### What are Microsoft AI Max URL inclusions?

They are controls that let eligible advertisers specify which website pages AI Max can use for landing destinations and as input for ad text customisation and search term matching.

### Do URL inclusions improve organic rankings?

No. They are campaign controls for Microsoft Advertising and should not be described as an organic ranking factor.

### Can AI Max use page content to customise ads?

Microsoft says URL inclusions can guide the pages used as a resource for text customisation and search term matching.

### Should SEO teams be involved?

Yes. SEO teams can help identify the right pages for different search intents, flag outdated content and ensure landing pages answer the query clearly.

### Are URL inclusions available to every advertiser?

Microsoft's documentation notes eligibility requirements for AI Max and URL inclusion capabilities. Advertisers should check their account access and current Microsoft guidance.

**Editorial note:** The November 19 update concerns Microsoft Advertising. Do not conflate these paid campaign controls with organic Bing ranking signals or Google Search features.
