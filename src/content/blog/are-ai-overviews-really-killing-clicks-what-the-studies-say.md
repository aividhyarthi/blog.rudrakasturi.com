"AI Overviews are killing website traffic" is the most repeated line in SEO. What do the studies actually say? Here is a summary, with each number attached to who measured it.

## The numbers

| Study | What it found |
|---|---|
| **SparkToro** (Similarweb data, US, Jan–Apr 2026) | 68.01% of Google searches ended without a click, up from 60.45% in 2024. It links this to AI Overviews but does not isolate how much they caused. |
| **A randomized field experiment** (reported by Search Engine Journal) | AI Overviews cut organic clicks by **38%** on the searches where they appeared. The authors call the results exploratory. |
| **Seer Interactive** (2025) | Organic click-through rate for informational searches with AI Overviews fell **61%** since mid-2024. Searches *without* them also fell 41%, which hints that clicking is declining more broadly. |
| **Pew Research Center** | People clicked a result 8% of the time when an AI summary showed, versus 15% without one. |

## A more hopeful result

Seer's follow-up for the last quarter of 2025 found that for pages cited in AI Overviews, click-through rate fell 61% quarter over quarter, **but clicks barely moved**. The reason: impressions grew faster. Another study reported by Search Engine Journal found the clicks that were lost were **not lower-quality** than the clicks that remained.

## Why the studies disagree

They measure different things (click rate versus total clicks), different query types, and different time periods. SparkToro says AI Overviews contribute to the rise in no-click searches, but its method cannot cleanly separate them from other causes. Search Engine Land also notes AI Mode was still a small share of searches in early 2026, though Google reports fast growth.

## How to check your own site

Search Engine Journal's "Ask an SEO" advice is simple:

1. Make a list of keywords where AI Overviews appear and a similar list where they do not.
2. Compare click-through rate for both lists **before and after** AI Overviews appeared.
3. If only the first list dropped, AI Overviews are a likely cause.

## What to do about it

- Aim to be **cited inside** the AI answer, not only ranked below it. (See [how Reddit and other sources get cited](/reddit-and-ai-answers-what-the-citation-data-actually-says/).)
- Create pages worth clicking: original data, tools, deep guides and anything an AI summary cannot fully replace.
- Track **impressions** as well as clicks. Visibility has value even when the click does not happen.
