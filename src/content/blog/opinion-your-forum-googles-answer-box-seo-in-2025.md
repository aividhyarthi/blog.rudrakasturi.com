Structured data was supposed to **help search engines understand your site better**.  
Now it just helps them **display more of your content without sending you traffic**.

Google says:

> **"Add this markup. Help us help you."**

But in reality?  
**You add markup. They take the content. Users get answers. You get... nothing.**

## DiscussionForumPosting – Just a Cleaner Way to Eat Your Community

You spent years building a forum.

Real people. Real opinions. Real threads with depth.  
And now Google wants you to label each part:

*   The question
*   Each reply
*   The upvotes
*   Who said what
*   When they said it

All in structured form.

Why?  
So they can pick the best parts and **show them directly in search**.  
Preview cards. AI summaries. “Helpful” snapshots.  
Everything _except the actual visit to your page_.

It’s like building a buffet and then watching the guests eat from the display window.

## QAPage – One Correct Answer. Zero Clicks.

QAPage is even worse.

Let’s say someone asks: _“How many calories in a banana?”_  
You mark it up, add the answer, author, timestamp – everything.

What happens?

Google shows the full answer in the search box itself.  
Sometimes even says _“According to yoursite.com…”_  
But the user?  
They scroll. They’re done. They never click.

Your content just became **a source citation**.

Congratulations. You’ve been turned into a footnote on your own article.

## Recipes. Reviews. Products. Forums. What’s Left?

This isn’t just about forums or Q&A.  
This is across the board.

*   `Recipe`: Full ingredient list in the SERP
*   `Review`: Your star rating, summary, even pros and cons – no visit needed
*   `Product`: Price, availability, seller – all visible up top
*   `HowTo`: Step-by-step breakdown, Google-hosted
*   `DiscussionForumPosting`: Community wisdom, repackaged

All perfectly tagged. All perfectly extractable.

What started as **helpful metadata** has now become **clean scraping instructions**.

## So What Does Google Want From You?

They want:

*   Your content
*   Your formatting
*   Your schema
*   Your traffic? Not so much.

It’s the classic platform play:

> **“Give us your structured data. We’ll give you visibility... maybe.”**

And publishers?  
We just keep doing it.  
Because if you don’t, you’re invisible.  
And if you do, you’re readable — _but not clickable._

## SEO in 2025 = Be Helpful Enough to Be Stolen

You’re no longer writing for readers.  
You’re feeding a machine that summarizes you faster than your own writer can finish their byline.

Google doesn’t need to kill your website.  
It just needs to **answer users before they reach it.**

And that’s exactly what structured data is doing now.  
At scale. With your help.

## So Do You Have a Choice?

No.  
You either add the markup and give Google the content in a nicely labelled box.  
Or you stay invisible and irrelevant.

You either become “eligible” for search visibility...  
Or you don’t exist at all.

## But Maybe, Just Maybe...

Maybe your content shows up in AI summaries.  
Maybe a few users click through.  
Maybe Google rewards clean markup with better rankings.

There’s always that **light at the end of the tunnel**, right?

Except no one knows if it’s hope...  
Or just the headlight of another algorithm update.

Either way – markup, smile, and keep feeding the machine.

Because in SEO, **what helps?**  
No one really knows.
