Google’s **Preferred Sources** feature lets you choose which publishers appear first for you in **Top Stories**. By adding us as your preferred source, you’ll see our updates more often and in a dedicated “From your sources” section.

![Screenshot of Google Search interface showing the 'Choose your preferred sources' popup for selecting news publishers.](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Screenshot-2025-08-13-at-8.33.37-AM-1024x584.webp)

### Step-by-Step Guide

1.  **Search for any trending topic** in Google Search.  
    For example: type “Latest News India” or “Technology News India” into the search bar.
2.  **Look for the “Top Stories” section.**  
    This usually appears near the top of the results page for news-related searches.
3.  **Tap or click the star icon** next to the “Top Stories” heading.
    *   On desktop, the star appears on the right side of the “Top Stories” header.
    *   On mobile, it appears in the same header area.
4.  **Choose your preferred sources.**
    *   A list of news sites will appear.
    *   Scroll to find **Rudra Kasturi** and tap the star next to it.
    *   You can select multiple sources.
5.  **Enjoy personalized news.**  
    Once added, stories from your preferred sources will:
    *   Appear more frequently in Top Stories
    *   Sometimes be shown in a dedicated “From your sources” carousel

### Pro Tips

*   If you don’t see the star, try searching a different trending news topic.
*   The feature is currently available for English searches in the **US** and **India**
