## The Big Update

Collaboration has always been one of the most powerful ways to grow on YouTube. When two or more creators come together, they don’t just share screen time they share audiences.

Now, YouTube has made this process much easier. With the new **Collaborations feature** in YouTube Studio, creators can directly add up to **five collaborators** to a single video.

## How It Works

*   Upload a video in YouTube Studio.
*   Invite up to **five other creators** to collaborate.
*   Once confirmed, the video will appear not only on your channel but also in the feeds of your collaborators’ audiences.
*   Revenue from the video goes to the **channel that posted it** (so ownership is clear).

This means your content gets exposed to new viewers who already trust and follow your collaborators.

## Why It Matters

1.  **Audience Sharing**  
    Collaborations are a proven way to grow faster. This update makes it smoother to introduce yourself to fresh audiences.
2.  **Discoverability**  
    Instead of viewers stumbling upon collab content randomly, YouTube now pushes it to both creators’ audiences.
3.  **Easier Workflow**  
    No more messy coordination or endless promotion. Adding collaborators happens right inside YouTube Studio.
4.  **Clear Revenue Ownership**  
    To avoid confusion, only the uploader’s channel gets ad revenue - keeping things transparent.

## Example Use Cases

*   A fashion YouTuber partners with a skincare creator for a combined tutorial.
*   Two gamers record a session together and publish it with shared exposure.
*   A travel vlogger and a food vlogger team up in the same city to cross-promote.

## Point To Note

Collabs have always been about creativity and community - now YouTube is making them **productized**.

This feature lowers the barrier for creators to grow together, making collaborations as easy as uploading.

If you’re a creator in 2025, think of collaborations not as occasional “special videos,” but as a **growth strategy**.

Because when creators share the stage, they also share success.
