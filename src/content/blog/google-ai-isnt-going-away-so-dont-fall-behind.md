### John Mueller’s Thoughts on AI

In a recent post on LinkedIn, John Mueller shared his views on the future of Artificial Intelligence (AI). He made it clear that **"AI is not going to go away."** He also warned that if you want to work in tech, you can’t ignore AI. As he put it, **"You can't be 'I hate AI and will never use it' if you want to work in a tech-related field."** This means that whether you like AI or not, it’s something you’ll need to work with if you’re involved in technology.

### The AI Hype: What’s Next?

John believes that the buzz around AI will eventually settle down. He said, **"The hype _will_ die down a bit, people will get tired of it, and some of the stupid uses of AI will hopefully be reduced."** However, he also mentioned that many amazing advancements in AI are here to stay and will continue to evolve. This means that while some of the current excitement might fade, AI itself will keep growing and improving in useful ways.

### Keep an Open Mind About AI

John’s message is not just about the future, but also advice for today. He encourages everyone to stay open to AI, even if you don’t like how it works right now. He said, **"We need to be more accepting of it, even if we don’t like how it is working right now."** This is because AI is still evolving, and it’s important to experiment with it to understand how it works. His advice is simple: **"We should continue to play with it, see how it works, and experiment with it so we are not left behind."**

### The Hype Can Be Overwhelming

John also talked about how overwhelming the hype around AI can be. **"There’s wild & crazy hype being pushed by many of the makers/sellers/promoters of the tools,"** he explained. This can make people feel insecure or unsure because it seems like AI is being overhyped. But John shared how he managed to handle this pressure. **"It settled down a bit once I had a chance to work out how these technologies work,"** he said. Understanding how AI works made it feel less like "magic" and more like something he could handle.

### Learning to Separate Hype from Reality

John admitted that it wasn’t easy to separate what’s real and useful from the hype. He said, **"The pressure from the hype is immense, and even after removing the hype, things are evolving quickly."** This means that even after you cut through the hype, AI is still moving fast, and it’s important to keep up.

### Understanding AI: The Key to Confidence

John’s main point is that understanding AI can make you feel more secure about it. He knows that not everyone has the time or background to dig into how these systems work. For those who can’t, he recommends finding trusted people who can help explain things clearly. **"If understanding how the systems work is too much, I’d try to find some trusted folks who can help you to see what’s behind the smoke,"** he said.

### Final Thoughts on AI

In his post, John also reflected on the excitement around AI tools like Bing Chat, Google SGE, and ChatGPT. He remembered how there was a lot of buzz when these tools came out but pointed out that recently things seem to be calming down a bit. **"In the past several months, things do seem to be calming a bit — at least with all the hype,"** he said.

### My Takeaway:

John Mueller’s message is clear: AI is here to stay. While the initial excitement might fade, the technology itself will keep advancing. Whether we like it or not, we should remain open to AI, keep learning about it, and not let ourselves fall behind.
