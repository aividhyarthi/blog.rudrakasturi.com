YouTube has announced its **largest update ever for YouTube Live**, and it’s going to change the way creators stream, earn, and connect with their audience.

**On average, over 30% of daily logged-in YouTube viewers watched live content in Q2 2025 alone**

**Why is this big for India?**

YouTube is India’s **largest digital platform with ~491 million monthly active users**. Globally, YouTube reports that **over 30% of logged-in users watch Live daily**. If we apply that to India, that means **150 million+ Indians are already watching Live content every day** from cricket and music concerts to devotional streams and gaming.

If you’re a creator in India, these tools are not optional anymore. Live is where your audience is spending time. Let’s break down the new features.

### Go Live With Confidence

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-79.webp)

**Practice Mode**  
You can now test your camera, mic, lighting, and full setup privately before going live. No more embarrassing “Am I audible?” starts.

**Playables on Live**  
A library of 75+ mini-games (trivia, puzzles, fun activities) is now available to make streams more interactive. Your viewers don’t just watch, they can play along.

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-80.webp)

### Get Discovered Faster

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-81.webp)

**Multi-Format Streaming**  
Stream both **horizontal and vertical formats at the same time**. This means your Live reaches big-screen TVs and desktop viewers, while also catering to the mobile-first audience in India. And the best part: the chat stays unified across both.

**React Live**  
Now you can stream your **reactions to other live events**. Whether it’s a cricket match, political rally, music launch, or trending video - creators can join the conversation in real time.

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-82.webp)

**AI-Powered Highlights**  
After your live ends, YouTube will automatically cut your **best moments into Shorts**. Perfect for creators who want to repurpose content quickly without spending hours editing.

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-83.webp)

### Earn More From Live

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-84.webp)

**Side-by-Side Ads**  
Ads now play _alongside_ your live stream, not interrupting it. This means smoother viewer experience and more earning potential for you.

**Switch to Members-Only Midstream**  
If you have channel memberships, you can now switch a public live into a **members-only stream** midway. Great for rewarding paying supporters with exclusive access.

### Why This Matters for Indian Creators

*   **150M+ Indians already watch Live daily** and that number is only growing.
*   Live streaming in India isn’t just for gamers. It’s huge for **music launches, devotional content, political events, cricket watch-alongs, comedy shows, and education streams**.
*   With these updates, YouTube is making it easier for creators to:
    *   Stream without stress.
    *   Reach audiences across every format.
    *   Earn more without disrupting the audience experience.

### Why You Should Care?

For Indian creators, this is a **turning point**.

Live is no longer just a fun add-on. It’s the **heartbeat of YouTube in India**. Whether you’re a singer, gamer, teacher, or entertainer, these new tools mean you can:

*   **Start confidently** (with practice mode).
*   **Grow faster** (through multi-format + AI highlights).
*   **Earn smarter** (via side-by-side ads + memberships).

In a country where YouTube is already replacing TV for millions, Live is your chance to own the stage.

Creators who embrace these updates will not just stream - they’ll **lead communities, shape conversations, and grow businesses**.
