Google recently made some updates to how **organization markup** is used and how they determine **title links** in search results. These changes might impact how your website appears on Google, especially when it comes to your logo and page titles. Let’s break down what’s changed, why it matters, and what you should do about it.

### **1\. Organization Markup: What’s Changed?**

**Google clarified** how **organization markup** works for websites, which can affect how your organization appears in search results—especially things like your logo and other key details about your business.

#### **What Is Organization Markup?**

**Organization markup** is a type of structured data that helps Google understand information about your company. This includes details like your business name, logo, contact information, and social media profiles. By adding this markup to your website, you can make sure Google presents your brand accurately in search results.

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/image-16-964x1024.png)

**Example**: Imagine a business page where you have a structured data entry that includes your company logo, name, and a description. Organization markup helps Google use this information effectively, making your brand look polished and consistent in search results.

#### **What’s the Update About?**

Google’s recent update is meant to **clarify** how organization markup is used:

*   **Influencing Logos**: Some properties in your organization markup, like the **URL to your logo**, can directly influence which logo Google displays next to your website name in search results.
*   **Behind-the-Scenes Use**: Other properties are used more in the background to help Google better understand your company and present relevant information about you. These aren’t directly visible but help improve how your brand appears in search.

#### **Why This Change Matters**

The update helps make it clearer what elements of your **[markup](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization)** have an **impact on visibility** versus those that are used for Google's internal understanding. This means that:

*   **You have more control**: By understanding which parts of the markup impact how your brand is presented, you can better control how your business looks in search.
*   **Clarity in Setup**: Knowing which elements matter for your brand’s visibility can help you prioritize and set up your structured data more effectively.
