Generative AI is no longer a niche tool. By mid-2025, **700 million people were using ChatGPT every week**. That equals **1 in 10 adults worldwide** - a pace of adoption faster than smartphones or the internet itself.

But here’s the real twist: of all these conversations, **1 in 4 are about seeking information**. In other words, ChatGPT is no longer just a writing partner - it is quietly replacing the way people search for facts, news, and advice.

### How ChatGPT Became a Daily Habit

ChatGPT’s weekly adoption growth is unprecedented:

*   Launched in **November 2022**.
*   By **July 2025**, it had reached **10% of the global adult population**.
*   Users send **2.6 billion daily messages**, or nearly **29,000 messages per second**.

That makes ChatGPT the fastest-growing consumer technology in history.

### The Rise of “Seeking Information”

In just one year:

*   **July 2024** → 14% of all messages were information-seeking.
*   **July 2025** → 24% of all messages were information-seeking.

That’s nearly **1 in 4 conversations** and it makes ChatGPT a direct competitor to Google, Wikipedia, and other traditional search platforms.

### Why This Matters

1.  **Google’s Turf Is at Risk**
    *   1 in 4 ChatGPT conversations directly replace a web search.
    *   Instead of typing queries into Google, people are asking AI for **personalized answers**.
2.  **Customization Is the Differentiator**
    *   Search gives you a link.
    *   ChatGPT gives you a tailored answer: _“Here’s a workout plan for a 40-year-old beginner runner with knee pain.”_
3.  **The Shift in Daily Behavior**
    *   For work: Writing dominates.
    *   For life: Information-seeking is the killer feature.
    *   This mirrors how smartphones shifted from communication to being **the primary gateway to the internet**.

### The India Context

*   India has seen **faster adoption in low- and middle-income segments**, according to the data.
*   With **half of ChatGPT’s global usage under the age of 26**, this mirrors India’s demographic dividend: young, tech-savvy, and increasingly using AI not just for jobs, but for learning and life.
*   In effect, India could be the world’s largest “AI-native” market.

### Metrics To Note

The numbers are clear:

*   **1 in 10 adults now talk to ChatGPT every week.**
*   **1 in 4 of those conversations are about seeking information.**

That means hundreds of millions of people are already using AI as their **first stop for knowledge**. This isn’t just adoption - it’s a fundamental rewiring of how humans interact with the internet.

The future of search isn’t links. It’s conversations.
