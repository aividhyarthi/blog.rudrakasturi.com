## Let’s be honest…

The SEO game we all knew has changed.

Back then:

> Rank on Google → get clicks → get leads → celebrate.

Now in 2025:

> AI gives the answer → user doesn’t even click → you don’t even know you helped.

So… what are we really measuring?

If your content is training AI or influencing AI answers — even if no one clicks — that _still has value_.  
That’s where these **12 new KPIs** come in.

## The 12 New KPIs You Need to Know in the GenAI + SEO World

### 1\. **Adoption Rate**

> Are people even using your AI tools (chatbots, smart search, etc.)?

Example:  
You built a chatbot on your site. Are visitors using it or skipping it?  
High adoption = people trust and prefer AI help.

### 2\. **Usage Frequency**

> How often do people use your GenAI features?

Daily? Weekly? Rarely?  
This tells you if it's just a gimmick or actually useful.

### 3\. **Session Length**

> How long do people spend during each interaction?

Are they just trying once and leaving? Or staying and asking multiple questions?  
More time = more value delivered.

### 4\. **Queries per Session**

> How many prompts/questions are they entering?

More queries = more engagement = your GenAI experience is doing its job.

### 5\. **Query Length**

> Are people typing short keywords or full questions?

In 2025, users don’t type “SEO tools.”  
They ask: _“Which is the best SEO tool for beginners in India under ₹500/month?”_  
This helps you design better content and prompt support.

### 6\. **Abandonment Rate**

> Are people quitting the experience mid-way?

Just like bounce rate in old SEO.  
If people leave without interacting much, your experience needs work.

### 7\. **User Satisfaction**

> Are people happy with the results?

This could be feedback after a chatbot session or thumbs up/down on GenAI responses.

### 8\. **Quality Index**

> Are your AI-generated answers accurate, helpful, and aligned with brand tone?

Especially important if you’re generating blogs or FAQs using AI.  
Bad quality = loss of trust.

### 9\. **Error Rate**

> How often is your AI giving wrong, outdated, or irrelevant answers?

Your content might be training the AI — but if the AI is wrong, it reflects on you too.  
Track this.

### 10\. **Latency**

> How fast is the AI responding?

Users today won’t wait 5 seconds for a chatbot reply.  
Slow = lost user.

### 11\. **Safety Score**

> Is your GenAI generating anything harmful, biased, or inappropriate?

This is critical if you’re deploying AI in public or user-facing platforms.

### 12\. **Business Impact Metrics**

> Are you saving money? Generating leads? Improving efficiency?

Example:

*   AI-generated content reduced your writer cost by 40%.
*   AI chatbot replaced 1 full-time support hire.

That’s ROI. That’s a KPI.

## But Rudra, What About My SEO Strategy?

Simple:  
If you’re only tracking **organic traffic**, you’re measuring with 1999 tools in a 2025 world.

Here’s the new mindset:

Old SEO KPI

GenAI-Era SEO KPI

Keyword Ranking

AI Mentions / Visibility

Organic Clicks

Influence in AI Responses

Bounce Rate

Abandonment Rate

Avg. Session

Queries Per Session

Backlinks

AI Trust + First-Party Data

## What Should Indian Founders and Marketers Actually Do?

*   **Embed AI into your content & customer journey**
*   **Track how people interact with it**
*   **Focus on _influence_, not just traffic**
*   **Build trust signals** — brand mentions, helpful content, consistent tone

## Final Thought

> “Your SEO strategy shouldn’t end at the search bar.  
> It should continue wherever the customer finds answers — even if that’s inside an AI.”

That’s the future.  
Start measuring what actually matters.
