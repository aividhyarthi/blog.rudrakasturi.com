Jad Tarifi, the founder of Google’s Generative AI team and a key mind behind the AI revolution we’re living through, didn’t mince words in his recent remarks.

He didn’t offer polite academic advice.

He issued a blunt warning.

> **“If you’re doing a PhD in AI now, it might be completely outdated before you’re even done.”**

And he didn’t stop there.

### **AI Degrees Themselves Might Become Redundant**

Tarifi says the _field of AI is moving so fast_, it could render its own degrees irrelevant. By the time a PhD student finishes their five-year research cycle, the core models, tools, and breakthroughs may have already changed.

> **“The AI you study now might not even exist when you finish.”**

In simpler terms: You’re chasing a moving train on foot.

### **Law, Medicine, and Other Knowledge-Heavy Fields Are Also at Risk**

He took a direct shot at the most traditional, high-prestige careers:

> **“Law and medicine degrees may become obsolete before you graduate. AI will solve many of those problems faster.”**

That’s not to say lawyers or doctors will disappear but that **AI will automate large parts of diagnosis, documentation, research, and routine casework**.

So if your education is focused purely on _memorizing rules, symptoms, or procedures_, AI might outdo you.

### **Don’t Get a PhD Just Because It’s Hot**

Tarifi didn’t say “never study.”  
But he did say: **Don’t chase AI just because it’s trending.**

> **“If you’re going into AI research because it’s hot you’re already late.”**

Translation: AI isn’t the stock market. You can’t ride the hype.  
You either bring a **deep, original question to solve** or get ready to be replaced by someone who does.

### **What Should You Do Instead? Go Narrow. Go Deep. Go Human.**

Tarifi’s advice for students was precise:

*   Don’t pursue generalist, prestige-chasing paths.
*   Focus on **underexplored intersections**: AI + biology, AI + education, AI + climate.
*   Develop what AI still can’t do: **empathy, intuition, creative judgment**.

He also emphasized: the next breakthroughs won’t come from the mainstream, but from **those who go deep into the edge cases** the boring problems, the regional languages, the messy real-world data.

### **The Real Quote That Should Shake Every College Classroom**

> **“The AI that replaces you is already being trained while you're stuck writing your thesis.”**

If that doesn’t cause a rethink in universities, career counselors, and student plans nothing will.

### In Summary

Tarifi’s warning is not about fear. It’s about **urgency**.

He’s not against education.  
He’s against **blindly following old maps in a world with new roads**.

So whether you're a student, parent, educator, or founder his message is clear:

*   **Rethink what you're learning.**
*   **Rethink why you're learning it.**
*   And most of all, **stop preparing for a future that no longer exists.**
