Google announced another set of Connected Apps for Search and Gemini on November 20, 2026. The new integrations are designed to help people complete tasks such as grocery shopping, checking credit information and estimating taxes without moving through a long chain of separate websites.

The announcement is currently focused on the United States. That distinction matters for Indian readers and businesses: the new partner list should not be described as an India-wide rollout.

## What Google announced

For grocery tasks, Google says users in the US can connect services including Albertsons, Safeway, Shaw's and Target. The integrations can help users build an order and check out.

Google also said Experian is coming to Search, allowing US users to check their credit rating and shop for a personal loan. H&R Block can help users calculate taxes and estimate refunds from Search.

Angi and Thumbtack integrations are expected in the coming weeks, helping users find local professionals for home services.

Google says the grocery integrations are powered by its Universal Commerce Protocol. The aim is to make it easier for an AI-powered search experience to connect a user with a service that can complete the next action.

## Search is moving closer to completing the task

For years, search engines helped users find a page where a task could be completed. A person searched for groceries, clicked a retailer, built a basket and paid on the retailer's website or app.

Connected Apps bring parts of that process into the search experience. The user can describe what they need, use an integrated service to assemble an order and move towards checkout.

This is a meaningful product direction. Search is not only answering questions or listing websites. It is becoming a place where users can discover services and initiate transactions.

## Why this matters to ecommerce and local businesses

If more users complete actions through connected services, retailers and service providers may need to think about how their product data, availability, prices and account connections are represented in these environments.

For ecommerce teams, accurate catalogues and inventory remain essential. A product recommendation is not useful if the item is unavailable or the price is outdated.

For local service businesses, the challenge is similar. Business details, service areas, availability and customer reviews need to be accurate wherever users discover the provider.

The announcement does not establish that every retailer or service provider will receive more orders through Google. Performance will depend on eligibility, integration quality, user adoption and the commercial terms of each partner.

## What Indian businesses should learn

The current announcement is US-focused, but Indian businesses can still learn from the direction of travel.

1. Keep product feeds, prices and inventory accurate.
2. Maintain consistent business information across websites and platforms.
3. Make booking, ordering and customer support flows easy to complete on mobile.
4. Review how structured product and business information is exposed to search platforms.
5. Treat AI search integration as an additional discovery and transaction channel, not a replacement for the company's own website or app.

Indian retailers should not assume that the named US partners or the same checkout experience are available in India today.

## The role of the Universal Commerce Protocol

Google identifies the Universal Commerce Protocol as the technology powering the grocery integrations. The wider idea is to create a more consistent way for AI experiences and commerce services to work together.

The important question for the market will be whether more merchants, platforms and payment providers adopt compatible integrations. A protocol becomes commercially meaningful when enough participants use it and when customers trust the resulting experience.

## What to watch

Watch the list of supported markets, participating merchants, checkout handoff, product availability accuracy and how transactions are attributed. Those details will determine whether connected search becomes a meaningful source of sales or remains a convenience feature for a limited set of partners.
