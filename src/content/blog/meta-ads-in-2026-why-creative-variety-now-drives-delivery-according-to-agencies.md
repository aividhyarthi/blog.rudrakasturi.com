If you run Facebook or Instagram ads, you have probably read that "creative is the new targeting". This article summarises what 2026 agency and vendor reports say, and flags what is unverified. We did not review Meta's own documentation, so treat the claims as practitioner views, not Meta statements.

## What the reports describe

- **Delivery relies on creative:** the reports say Meta's Andromeda delivery engine reads ad creative to decide who sees an ad, so targeting happens largely through the creative itself.
- **Fewer, broader ad sets:** several guides say over-segmenting audiences fragments signals and slows learning, and recommend consolidating ad sets.
- **Genuinely different creatives:** advisers recommend distinct hooks, angles and formats, not cosmetic variations. One guide suggests 8 to 12 distinct concepts per campaign.
- **Advantage+ defaults:** guides say broad audiences, automated placements and Advantage+ creative are now defaults for new campaigns.
- **Fewer edits:** sources agree that frequent edits disrupt learning, but differ on how long to wait, with figures of five to seven days.

## What we could not verify

Some posts cite performance gains from Meta's internal testing or from using Advantage+ creative, and one describes a "creative similarity" threshold that suppresses near-duplicate ads. We could not find these in Meta documentation, so we do not repeat the numbers. One source also says Meta has required disclosure of AI-generated or AI-modified ad content since March 2026. Check Meta's current ad policies before relying on that.

## How to test it

1. **Pick one campaign** and keep audiences broad, as a controlled test.
2. **Produce distinct concepts:** problem-led, proof-led, offer-led and how-it-works formats.
3. **Retire weak ads with a rule,** not on a hunch, and avoid constant edits.
4. **Track cost per result** against your current setup for a fixed period.
5. **Check policies** on AI-generated content and disclosures.
6. **Keep your own site ready:** ads send people to pages that should be fast and clear. See our guide to [answer-first pages](/write-answer-first-pages-open-with-the-direct-answer-then-the-detail/).
