Google Play Points launched in 2018 to reward users for apps, games, and digital content.

Now they’re **upgrading the program** in the UK with new perks, more rewards, and fun game-style experiences.

Here are the key updates:

## What’s New

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JpnBehgQhME

*   **Merchandise & collectibles**: You’ll be able to convert your Play Points into items like apparel, accessories, and more via partnerships with Android, YouTube, Chrome, and top developers.
*   **Game-style rewards**: _Diamond Valley_, a treasure hunt minigame (already in Korea, Japan, US), is coming to the UK in early November. Players can hunt diamonds and win prizes like Pixel devices, special merch, bonus points, etc.
*   **Early access & bonuses for higher tiers**: If you’re a Gold+ member, you’ll get early access (from Nov 3) and bonus diamonds. Then from Nov 10, the game opens to all UK Play users.

## Why It Matters

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-66.webp)

*   **More than just points**: It’s shifting from “collect points for apps” to “earn real, tangible stuff + fun experiences.” That’s more engaging.
*   **Game mechanics boost engagement**: Treasure hunts encourage more frequent interaction. Users who may only occasionally open Play could now come back just to earn rewards.
*   **Tiered benefits encourage loyalty**: Higher status = earlier access + more bonuses. Incentive to stay higher up in the rewards program.
*   **Good move for Google in UK market**: Competitive edge in digital content ecosystem; more reason for users to stay inside Google/Play ecosystem versus looking elsewhere.

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/image-67.webp)

## Details to Note

*   Rewards are rolling out over weeks.
*   Some perks are exclusive to higher tiers (Gold+), at least early on.
*   Play Points expire after 12 months. Must be 18+ to participate.

## Why You Should Care

Google Play Points in the UK is no longer just a passive rewards scheme. It’s being refashioned as an **active engagement engine** with real perks, games, and status benefits.

If you use Play Points, this update means more value for your time not just points, but things you can actually use + fun along the way.
