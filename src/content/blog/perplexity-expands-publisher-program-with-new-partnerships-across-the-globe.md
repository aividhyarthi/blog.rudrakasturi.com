Perplexity, the AI-powered search engine renowned for delivering factual and valuable answers, has [announced a significant expansion of its publisher program](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/perplexity-expands-publisher-program-with-15-new-media-partners). Prominent news organizations including the LA Times, Adweek, and Mexico News Daily have signed up alongside a dozen other outlets to participate in a program that promises to reshape how publishers interact with AI-driven search platforms.

Under this program, publishers will benefit from shared ad revenue generated on Perplexity and gain access to robust metrics to track the performance of their content. However, publishers must remain in the program to continue enjoying these benefits.

### **Fostering Collaboration with News Organizations**

“We would not be able to serve factual, valuable answers without news organizations continuing to report on different topics,” said Jessica Chan, head of publisher partnerships at Perplexity. “We’re excited to welcome these new publishers to the program.”

This statement underscores the critical role that credible news outlets play in fueling the AI engine behind Perplexity’s accurate and context-rich responses. The expanded program aims to establish a mutually beneficial relationship between the search engine and its publishing partners.

### **Who’s Joining the Program?**

The expansion welcomes a diverse range of publishers, reflecting global and industry-specific interests. The list includes:

*   **Spanish-language media brand Pris Media**
*   **Newspaper conglomerate Lee Enterprises**
*   **The Independent**
*   **Blavity** (a platform focusing on Black culture and communities)
*   **NewsPicks** (a curated business news platform)
*   **Minkabu Infonoid** (a Japanese finance and investment platform)
*   **Gear Patrol** (a lifestyle and product discovery platform)
*   **MediaLab**
*   **DPReview** (a photography and camera-focused publication)
*   **World History Encyclopedia**
*   **RTL Germany brands NTV and Stern**

### **A Step Toward Sustainable Journalism**

By sharing ad revenue with publishers, Perplexity aligns its financial success with the prosperity of its content contributors. This approach could pave the way for AI-driven platforms to support journalism in a meaningful way, especially as traditional revenue streams for news organizations face ongoing disruption.

In addition to ad revenue sharing, the program provides publishers with valuable insights into how their content performs on the platform. These metrics empower news organizations to fine-tune their strategies for content creation and audience engagement.

### **Looking Ahead**

With its expanded publisher program, Perplexity is setting a precedent for how AI-powered platforms can support the journalism ecosystem. The inclusion of major global outlets and niche media brands highlights Perplexity’s commitment to delivering accurate and diverse information to its users while ensuring that content creators are rewarded for their contributions.

As the program evolves, it will be interesting to see how this collaborative model shapes the future of AI-driven content delivery and strengthens the relationship between technology platforms and the journalism industry.
