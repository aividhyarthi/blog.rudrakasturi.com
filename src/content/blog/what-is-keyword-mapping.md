Mapping is a commonly used technique for Search Engine Optimisation that helps to allocate a set of target keywords to each specific page. This method has been employed more in the past, but it still holds a great relevance and marketers should not overlook this digital marketing technique. 

How does Keyword mapping work? When researching about keywords it can really become difficult. It can become extremely technical and fiddly. To delve into how this works it is important to grow through the basics. Keywords are simple words or a group of words that hold some relevance to your website. Now, this helps the search engine to know more about your website, what information it imparts, or various bucketing features to give the best search result. The procedure begins with assigning crucial or primary keywords to each page, followed by a list of secondary variants and supporting terms, which could have hundreds of combinations. 

Let me break this down for you! The purpose of a Google search analysis is to acknowledge the intent behind the search of the user. The secret skill to understand the user’s search intent is keyword mapping. If your used keywords are the best answer to their search intent, Google will find you most relevant and directly increase the rankings and traffic that you desire.

So, what's new, and why is keyword mapping important for your website’s SEO success in 2021? Keyword mapping remains one of the most critical and most used techniques for on-page optimisation. Examining the SEO-friendliness, readability, and consistency of your text has always been an important trick for increasing traffic and engagement by increasing the number of people that visit your site. If this part of your website is taken care of it can lead to great results in Ranking and directing more traffic to your website. 

There is always a need to constantly take up keyword mapping in order to attain top ranking. Choosing of the keywords for the website should be done based on multiple factors and are competitive. However, as an SEO practitioner you should always remember every keyword is extremely ‘competitive’ in the web domain. Everybody offering a similar product and service will use similar, same, or similar combination of keywords for targeting the right audience. The key to deal with such competition is to avoid long-tailed keywords that means your keywords should not be more than three words. Keeping the keywords product/service specific and not too many can help you succeed on Google Keyword Mapping Technology.
