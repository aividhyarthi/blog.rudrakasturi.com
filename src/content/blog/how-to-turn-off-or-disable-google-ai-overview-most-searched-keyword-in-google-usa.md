### **Why Are People Breaking Up with Google AI Overviews?**

Google AI Overview is part of its _Search Generative Experience (SGE)_—a feature that summarizes search results using AI. While it’s designed to save time, many users feel:

*   It clutters the search page.
*   It overshadows traditional organic links.
*   It sometimes misses the mark with incomplete or irrelevant answers.
*   It takes away the joy of exploring multiple sources.

For some, it feels like an unwanted guest at the dinner table—one that talks too much and doesn’t let others speak!

### **How to Remove or Get Rid of Google AI Overview (Tailored for You)**

Now, let’s dive into solutions. Whether you’re a tech wizard or someone who just wants simple fixes, I’ll guide you step by step.

#### **If You’re Tech-Savvy: Go the Advanced Route**

**Use Chrome Extensions** Extensions are your best friend if you know your way around browsers:

*   Install **Hide Google AI Overviews** or **Bye Bye, Google AI** from the Chrome Web Store.
*   These extensions remove AI-generated panels and even clean up other clutter like sponsored links. _Pro Tip_: These work on both Chrome and Edge browsers.

**Tweak Your Browser Settings** Want to bypass AI Overviews completely? Here’s how:

*   Open Chrome and type `chrome://settings/searchEngines`.
*   Add a new search engine with:
    *   **Name**: AI-Free Search
    *   **Shortcut**: @cleansearch
    *   **URL**: `{google:baseURL}search?q=%s&udm=14`
*   Set this as your default search engine for a cleaner experience.

**Fiddle with Experimental Features**If you’re enrolled in Google Labs:

*   Visit [Google Labs](https://labs.google.com).
*   Disable “AI Overviews” under Search Labs settings.
*   This minimizes their presence but doesn’t fully remove them.

* * *

#### **If You’re a Regular Search User: Keep It Simple**

**Switch to Alternative Filters**  
On Google Search, try using the “Web” filter located under the search bar. This often displays traditional results without AI summaries.

**Try Tenbluelinks.org** For mobile users who can’t install extensions:

*   Visit tenbluelinks.org.
*   Follow their instructions to set up a custom search engine that avoids AI Overviews.

**Explore Other Search Engines**  
If all else fails, consider switching to alternatives like Bing or DuckDuckGo that don’t have similar AI-generated panels.
