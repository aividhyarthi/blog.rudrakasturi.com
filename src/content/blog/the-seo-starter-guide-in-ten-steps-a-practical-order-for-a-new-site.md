Google's SEO Starter Guide (last updated 10 December 2025 as we read it) is long and covers a lot. Its section headings give a sensible order, and we have grouped them into ten steps. The grouping is ours. The advice behind each step is Google's.

**Best for:** anyone launching a new site or blog.

![Ten steps grouped into four stages: be found, be organised, be useful, look good in results](/images/seo-starter-guide-ten-steps/ten-steps.svg)

## The ten steps

**A. Be found**
1. **Understand how Search works.** Google finds, analyses and serves pages. See [how Google Search works](/how-google-search-works-crawl-index-and-serve-and-where-a-page-gets-stuck/).
2. **Help Google find your content.** Links between pages and a sitemap. See [XML sitemaps](/xml-sitemaps-for-a-blog-what-to-include-and-how-to-submit/).
3. **Check Google sees what users see.** Use the URL Inspection tool, and keep key content out of script-only rendering. See [JavaScript SEO check](/javascript-seo-a-ten-minute-check-for-script-heavy-sites/).

**B. Be organised**
4. **Use descriptive URLs and group related pages.** Readable words, sensible folders.
5. **Reduce duplicate content.** See [canonical URLs](/canonical-urls-for-a-blog-three-signals-and-six-mistakes-to-avoid/).

**C. Be useful**
6. **Make the page interesting and useful.** Expect your readers' search terms, and avoid distracting ads.
7. **Link to relevant resources.** Send readers to sources and related pages.

**D. Look good in results**
8. **Influence your title links and snippets.** See [title links](/how-google-chooses-your-title-link-and-how-to-write-one/) and [snippets](/snippets-and-meta-descriptions-what-google-says-and-how-to-write-them/).
9. **Add images and video well.** Place images near relevant text and add descriptive alt text.
10. **Promote the site and keep learning.** Google also lists things it believes you should not focus on.

## What Google says about timing

The guide has a section on how long it takes to see impact in results. We did not find a promise of speed, and Google says elsewhere that it does not guarantee crawling, indexing or serving. Treat any promise of fast results with caution.

## A worked example (made-up)

A new recipe blog has 12 posts, no sitemap, and the same title on every page. Under our order, step 2 (sitemap) and step 8 (titles) are the first fixes, and rewriting the recipes can wait.

## What to do

1. Score yourself from 1 to 10 on each step this week.
2. Fix the lowest score in group A first, then B, C and D.
3. Re-check after changes with the URL Inspection tool.
4. Use [our traffic drop routine](/search-traffic-drop-a-four-step-debugging-routine-from-googles-guide/) if numbers fall later.
