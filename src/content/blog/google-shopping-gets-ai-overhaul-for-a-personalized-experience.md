Google has revamped its shopping platform using cutting-edge artificial intelligence (AI) to transform how users shop online. The new AI-powered Google Shopping, rolling out in the U.S., integrates Google’s Shopping Graph, which features 45 billion product listings, with AI models like Gemini to offer a more personalized and streamlined experience. Here’s a breakdown of the key updates:

### 1\. **AI-Powered Research Assistance**

The biggest improvement comes in the form of AI-generated briefs that help shoppers with product research. The new system intelligently summarizes relevant details for your search, giving you quick insights on what to consider before making a purchase.

For instance, if you search for a “Men’s winter jacket for Seattle,” Google Shopping will now generate a brief tailored to the Pacific Northwest climate. This brief provides key factors to consider, such as waterproof material and warmth level, and highlights top-recommended products based on reviews from across the web. Users can also click through to related articles for a deeper dive into the topic.

Moreover, dynamic filters help users refine their search based on specific needs, like size or availability, making the shopping process smoother. With AI-driven tools like virtual try-on powered by augmented reality (AR), shoppers can visualize clothing items, adding an extra layer of confidence before purchasing.

### 2\. **Personalized Shopping Feed**

Google Shopping now offers a personalized home page, featuring shoppable products and videos based on individual user preferences. This feed keeps track of your shopping behavior, allowing you to continue your research seamlessly over several days or weeks. Simply click on the Google Shopping logo or visit shopping.google.com to access your personalized feed.

In addition, users have the flexibility to update or turn off personalized results by managing preferences in the shopping menu, ensuring the platform respects individual privacy and choices.

### 3\. **Deal-Finding Tools for the Best Prices**

Google knows that finding the best price is crucial for shoppers, and the new Shopping platform comes equipped with powerful deal-finding tools. Price comparison, price insights, and price tracking features help users get the best deals at any time.

![](https://storage.googleapis.com/gweb-uniblog-publish-prod/images/Deals-4up.width-500.format-webp.webp)

A newly introduced **dedicated deals page** makes it easier to discover discounts curated specifically for you. This page shows personalized deals, along with the latest available discounts from various stores, all in one place.

### 4\. **Enhanced Shopping for All Stores**

Google Shopping aims to provide a seamless shopping experience for every store. Whether you’re browsing for clothes or electronics, the new AI-powered system connects shoppers with rich product listings from all types of brands. Google is also expanding how brands can reach shoppers directly through AI-enhanced shopping ads.

### 5\. **User Feedback and Experimentation**

One of the standout aspects of this revamped platform is its focus on continuous improvement. Since the AI-generated briefs are still in an experimental phase, Google encourages users to provide feedback via the three-dot menu on the brief. This feedback will help refine and optimize the system over time.

You can experience this new AI-powered shopping experience by visiting Google’s Shopping Tab or going directly to shopping.google.com. The rollout will continue in the U.S. over the coming weeks, making this AI-powered transformation accessible to millions of users.

### Google’s new AI-driven shopping platform marks a major leap in how people shop online. With personalized recommendations, smarter research tools, virtual try-ons, and powerful deal-finding features, the platform offers a seamless, tailored shopping experience. As Google continues to gather user feedback, the experience is set to become even better, ensuring that shoppers get what they need quickly and efficiently.
