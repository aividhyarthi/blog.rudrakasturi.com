**Quick question:**  
If you're creating content in 2025, should you focus more on images, videos, or just plain old text?

Here's the truth: AI doesn't consume content like humans do. It doesn’t “watch,” “see,” or “feel.” It **reads**, **parses**, and **summarizes**. That means not all formats are treated equally.

Let’s break it down.

## 1\. **Tables Are an LLM’s Best Friend**

If your blog or webpage includes a **clean, well-labeled table**, congratulations — you just gave the AI a shortcut.

Why tables work:

*   They help AI instantly extract relationships
*   They’re treated like structured data (think: CSV or JSON)
*   Great for comparisons, pricing, top 10 lists, pros and cons

**How to optimize:**

*   Label rows and columns clearly
*   Keep structure consistent
*   Add a short explanation above or below

> **_TL;DR: If it’s in a table, AI can reuse it._**

## 2\. **Images? Only If You Add Context**

Here’s the problem: AI can’t “see” your JPEG.

Unless it’s a multimodal model (like GPT-4o or Gemini 1.5 Pro), your image is invisible. What it _can_ understand is:

*   The **alt text**
*   The **caption**
*   The **surrounding paragraph**

**How to optimize:**

*   Always add descriptive ALT text
*   Place a one-liner summary below the image
*   If it’s a chart or infographic, explain the insight in plain English

> **_Don’t post naked images. Dress them with words._**

## 3\. **Videos? Only If You Type It Out**

Embedding a YouTube video? Great for humans.  
Completely useless for AI – unless you **give it words to chew on**.

AI doesn’t “watch” your video. It skims your page looking for:

*   Transcripts
*   Timestamps
*   Summaries
*   Descriptive headings
*   Schema markup like `VideoObject`

**How to optimize:**

*   Add a written summary below the video
*   Use bullet points to highlight key moments
*   Mention the topic in the heading (H2 or H3)
*   Use schema if you’re serious about rankings

> **_AI needs subtitles. Always._**

## 4\. **Bullets, Headings, and Structure: Crucial**

LLMs don’t scroll. They navigate. That’s why **your structure matters.**

They love:

*   H1, H2, H3 that are logically arranged
*   Bullet points (like this)
*   FAQs at the bottom of the page
*   Short paragraphs with topic keywords

**How to optimize:**

*   Use semantic HTML properly
*   Keep each section under 150 words
*   Add a TL;DR or key takeaways box

> **_Treat your blog like a table of contents for AI._**

## 5\. **The Hidden Boost: Schema Markup**

AI loves metadata.

Adding the right schema:

*   Helps Google AI Overviews “understand” your content better
*   Gets you featured as a citation
*   Improves contextual accuracy in summaries

Start with:

*   `Article`
*   `FAQPage`
*   `HowTo`
*   `VideoObject` (for video pages)

**You don’t need to be technical. Tools like RankMath or Yoast can help you add schema easily.**

If you’re creating content in 2025, ask yourself:

> **_“Can an AI summarize this without having to guess?”_**

Because guess what?

Give it what it wants – **not what looks pretty.**
