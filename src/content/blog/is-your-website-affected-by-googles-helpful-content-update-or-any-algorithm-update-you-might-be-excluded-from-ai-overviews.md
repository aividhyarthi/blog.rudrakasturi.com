In the ever-evolving landscape of SEO and content creation, Google’s Helpful Content Update (HCU) and core algorithm updates have reshaped how content is ranked and presented. Recently, a fascinating observation by industry experts has surfaced: websites hit by the Helpful Content Update seem to be excluded from Google’s AI Overviews, even if they rank well in traditional search results.

This raises critical questions about what it means for your website and how Google evaluates content. Let's break this down and explore why producing high-quality, user-focused content is more important than ever.

* * *

### **What is Google's Helpful Content Update?**

Google’s Helpful Content Update was introduced to reward websites that provide useful, people-first content, while penalizing those that produce content primarily for search engines. The update, now a part of the broader core updates, focuses on eliminating low-quality, AI-generated, or overly optimized content that doesn’t add real value to users.

In simple terms, Google is saying: “If your content is not helpful to users, your rankings will suffer.”

* * *

### **AI Overviews and the Exclusion of Penalized Sites**

Industry experts like Lily Ray and Glenn Gabe have noticed an intriguing pattern: sites affected by the Helpful Content Update are often missing from Google’s AI Overviews. Even when these sites rank well in traditional search results, they are excluded from AI-generated summaries.

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/image-37.png)

For example, when users ask Google AI Overview about a site that has been penalized by the HCU, they see information from other sites talking about the penalized site, but not from the site itself.

This suggests there could be a **filter** in place that excludes sites impacted by the Helpful Content Update from AI Overviews. Although Google hasn’t officially confirmed this, it is becoming increasingly clear that AI-generated content and traditional SEO rankings are both affected by core updates.

* * *

### **Why This Matters: Two Big Reasons**

If Google is indeed filtering penalized sites from AI Overviews, there are two major implications:

1.  **Using AI-Generated Content from Penalized Sites Can Lead to Inaccurate Results**  
    Google’s AI Overview aims to provide accurate, high-quality information. Including content from sites that have been penalized by the Helpful Content Update could lead to incorrect or misleading information. As a result, it makes sense that Google would filter out such sites to maintain the quality of its AI responses.
2.  **It’s a Signal to Users and Websites About Content Quality**  
    The exclusion of penalized sites from AI Overviews can be seen as an indicator that these sites are not delivering the best content. This not only impacts their AI Overview ranking but can also damage their traditional SEO rankings. In the long run, sites producing unhelpful or AI-manipulated content will find it difficult to rank well in any context—AI or traditional search.

* * *

### **The Industry Insight: What Experts Are Saying**

As Lily Ray noted, even if a site ranks first in traditional search results, it may still be excluded from AI Overviews if it has been penalized by the Helpful Content Update. Glenn Gabe’s research supports this, revealing that even sites that have shown some recovery after the August core update are still missing from AI Overviews.

This leads to a critical takeaway for anyone involved in SEO or content creation: **user-focused content is non-negotiable**. The days of creating content just to appease algorithms are over. Google’s updates are designed to ensure that only the best, most useful content gets ranked—whether in traditional search results or AI Overviews.

* * *

### **What Does This Mean for Your SEO Strategy?**

If you’re hoping to rank in Google’s AI Overviews and traditional search, here’s what you need to focus on:

**Focus on Long-Term Quality**  
Don’t get caught up in quick fixes to manipulate rankings. Instead, aim for long-term, user-first content strategies that align with Google’s evolving standards.

**Create Content for Users, Not Just Algorithms**  
Google’s goal is clear: prioritize content that helps users. Focus on answering user queries thoroughly, providing valuable insights, and maintaining authenticity.

**Avoid Low-Quality, AI-Generated Content**  
AI-generated content can be useful, but it must be of high quality and genuinely helpful to users. Sites using AI solely to churn out content for SEO purposes risk being penalized.

**Keep an Eye on Core Updates**  
Google frequently updates its core algorithm, and the Helpful Content Update is now baked into these updates. Be sure to monitor your site’s performance during these times and adjust your strategy accordingly.

### **My Takeaway: Content Quality is Key for Both AI and Traditional SEO Rankings**

Google’s AI Overviews and traditional search rankings are more intertwined than ever, and both are impacted by core updates, including the Helpful Content Update. While it's not yet confirmed by Google that penalized sites are being excluded from AI Overviews, industry experts have observed this trend. As search evolves, the takeaway for SEO professionals is simple: focus on quality content that serves users. By doing so, you’ll not only improve your traditional SEO rankings but also stand a better chance of being included in AI Overviews.

In short, don’t create content just for bots—create content that helps users, and the bots will follow.
