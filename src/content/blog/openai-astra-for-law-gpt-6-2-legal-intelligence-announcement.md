## A specialised legal configuration, not just a general chatbot

OpenAI announced an updated Astra for Law configuration on November 17, 2026. It is built on GPT 6.2 Astra and combines the model with an expanded legal search index and instructions for researching legal questions, weighing authorities and drafting answers.

OpenAI says the configuration ranked first on Harvey's LAB-AA evaluation and performed strongly on other legal research benchmarks. The company is making access requestable by ChatGPT Enterprise customers through its Trusted Access Program.

## What the benchmark says

Harvey's Legal Agent Benchmark tests whether a model can complete legal assignments across areas such as deposition preparation, commercial lease review and acquisition strategy.

OpenAI says Astra for Law earned full credit on LAB-AA assignments nearly 40% more often than the runner-up, GPT 6.2 Astra. The company also reports 95% fewer hallucinations per assignment than Claude Opus 5.5.

These are benchmark results reported by OpenAI and its evaluation partners. They are not proof that a model can replace a qualified lawyer or that it will perform equally well in every jurisdiction.

## Why the specialised configuration matters

Legal work depends on current authorities, jurisdiction, procedural context and careful citation. A general model may produce fluent language but still miss a controlling case or misstate the status of a rule.

OpenAI says Astra for Law combines GPT 6.2 Astra with a legal search index that has expanded to more than 310 million URLs. The company says the index grew by 34% since September.

The configuration is designed to support research, argument testing, drafting and workflow preparation. Lawyers remain responsible for checking sources, interpreting the law and advising clients.

## What law firms should test

A useful pilot should compare the system against real, closed matters where the correct answer is already known. Test whether citations exist, whether the authority is current, whether contrary authority is identified and whether the model distinguishes binding from persuasive sources.

Measure the rate of unsupported legal claims, missing authorities, incorrect citations and time saved after lawyer review.

Do not evaluate only writing quality. In legal work, a polished but incorrect answer is a serious failure.

## Availability

OpenAI says ChatGPT Enterprise customers can request access through the Trusted Access Program. The API access path is also described in the announcement. Availability should be confirmed with the organisation's administrator.

## The larger point

The direction is clear: specialised AI systems are being built around professional workflows, search indexes and domain-specific evaluation rather than relying only on general model capability.

For legal teams, the opportunity is faster research and better preparation. The responsibility is to retain professional review, source verification and client confidentiality.
