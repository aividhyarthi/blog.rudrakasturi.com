**Quick answer:** AEO means shaping your pages so AI tools can find them, understand them and cite them when they answer a question. It sits on top of SEO. It does not replace it.
**Best for:** site owners, marketers and writers who keep hearing "AEO" and want to know what to do about it.

## AEO in one table

| Term | The goal | Where it shows |
|---|---|---|
| **SEO** (search engine optimization) | A ranked link | Google and Bing results |
| **AEO** (answer engine optimization) | Your content is the answer | AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Copilot |
| **GEO** (generative engine optimization) | Your brand is named in a written answer | AI Mode, Perplexity |
| **ASO** (app store optimization) | An app is found and installed | App Store, Google Play |

The labels overlap. Sources use AEO, GEO and "AI SEO" for close to the same job, and there is no settled rulebook. Our explainer on the terms is [AEO, GEO and AI SEO](/aeo-geo-ai-seo-what-the-terms-mean-and-what-google-actually-says/).

## How an AI answer is built

Google describes two techniques behind its generative AI features. Retrieval-augmented generation fetches relevant pages through its ranking systems, and the model uses them to write a response with links. Query fan-out runs several related searches at once to collect more material.

So the first gate is ordinary SEO. Google says a page must be indexed and eligible to show with a snippet to appear in these features.

![The five steps of an AI answer: question, related searches, fetched pages, written answer, linked sources](/images/what-is-aeo/how-ai-answers-are-built.svg)

## What Google says to do

According to Google's guide, as reported by Search Engine Journal, AEO and GEO are not separate from SEO. Google also says you do not need an llms.txt file, special AI markup, or content cut into small pieces. It points to original, helpful content and sound technical basics instead.

Our view: the part within your control is clarity. Put the direct answer in the first lines, name the thing you are explaining, and keep one topic per page.

## What a good AEO page looks like (our view)

Use the same pattern that works for guides that rank today: one question, the answer first, a table, then the same short block for each part.

| Page part | Weak version | AEO-ready version |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | Three paragraphs of background | A two-line answer to the title question |
| Facts | Buried in prose | A table or list a reader can scan |
| Proof | General claims | A test, number or example only you have |
| Questions | None | The real follow-up questions, answered briefly |

## A 10-minute test

1. List five questions your page should answer.
2. Ask each one in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Copilot.
3. Record whether your site is cited, mentioned or absent.
4. Repeat monthly. Use the [20-prompt AI visibility test](/a-20-prompt-ai-visibility-test-template-scoring-rubric-and-monthly-routine/).

AI answers vary by user and by day, so treat one result as a sample, not a rank.

## Common questions

**Is AEO different from SEO?** Google says no. Others argue mentions on other sites also matter. Both can be true.

**Do I need schema for AEO?** Not as an AI trick. See [schema by page type](/schema-for-aeo-and-geo-a-decision-table-by-page-type-and-a-ten-point-audit/).

**Can AEO guarantee citations?** No. Nobody controls what an AI tool shows.

## What to do

1. Confirm your key pages are indexed and can show a snippet.
2. Rewrite each opening as a direct answer. See [answer-first pages](/write-answer-first-pages-open-with-the-direct-answer-then-the-detail/).
