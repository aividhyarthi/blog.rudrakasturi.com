**Connecting the World Through Language**

Ever wanted to learn something new from a creator in another country but struggled with language barriers? YouTube's new **Auto Dubbing** feature is here to make global content more accessible than ever! Whether you're learning to cook a French delicacy, mastering sewing from an Indian artisan, or exploring eerie travel spots worldwide, **auto dubbing** lets you enjoy videos in your language seamlessly.

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/image-44.png)

### **How Does It Work?**

Follow these easy steps to get started with auto dubbing:

#### **1\. Upload Your Video**

🎥 Just upload your video as usual—nothing extra needed!  
YouTube automatically detects the language of your content and creates dubbed versions in other languages.

#### **2\. Review and Manage Your Dubbed Videos**

🛠️ Want to check your dubs? Go to **YouTube Studio** and head to the **"Languages" section**.  
Here, you can:

*   Preview your dubbed videos.
*   Unpublish or delete any dubs you don’t like.

#### **3\. Enable Auto Dubbing in YouTube Studio**

💻 Not sure if auto dubbing is available for your channel?  
Visit the **"Advanced Settings"** in YouTube Studio.  
If available, review your dubs before making them live!

### **Languages Supported**

*   **From English** to: French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Portuguese, and Spanish.
*   **To English** from the same supported languages above.

Viewers can easily switch between the original language and the auto-dubbed track using the **track selector**, and YouTube remembers their preference for future videos.

### **Things to Note**

*   This technology is still evolving, so dubbing may not always perfectly capture the speaker’s tone or context.
*   Feedback is crucial to help us improve accuracy and make dubs sound even more natural!

### **What’s Next?**

We’re working with **Google DeepMind** and **Google Translate** to bring exciting new updates, including:

*   **Expressive Speech:** Dubs that mimic the creator's tone, emotion, and surroundings for a more immersive experience.
