Google is now showing **AI-generated summaries** right inside Discover.

Earlier: Title, thumbnail, maybe a line.  
Now: Google read articles, watches the video, **summarizes it**, and gives users a ready-to-skim AI blurb.

No click. No visit. No traffic for you.
