If you’ve ever wondered whether deleting old blog posts or pruning pages from your website will boost your Google rankings, here’s the simple answer: **It depends**. Let’s break it down with insights from Google’s Search Liaison, Danny Sullivan.

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/image-10-1024x768.png)

### **1\. What’s “Crawl Budget”? (And Should You Care?)**

*   **Crawl Budget** = How often Googlebot visits and indexes your site’s pages.
*   **For Giant Sites**: If you’re Amazon or Wikipedia, optimizing crawl budget matters. Googlebot needs to prioritize which pages to crawl first.
*   **For Smaller Sites**: Danny Sullivan says, _“**If your site is not gigantic, then I wouldn’t worry about this.”**_

### **2\. When Does Pruning Help?**

Pruning (removing pages) can improve SEO **only in specific cases**:

*   **Duplicate Content**:_“If it’s around removing duplication of pages (20 of the same pages), then yes.”_
    *   Example: Deleting 10 identical product pages for the same shirt frees Googlebot to focus on unique content.
*   **Low-Quality Pages**: Remove thin, outdated, or irrelevant content to strengthen your site’s overall authority.

### **3\. When Does Pruning Hurt?**

*   **Deleting Good Content**: Removing helpful, traffic-driving articles can backfire.
*   **Broken Links**: Forgetting to redirect deleted pages (use **301 redirects**) leads to 404 errors, harming user experience.

### **4\. Danny Sullivan’s Advice for Small Sites**

*   _“Focus on quality, not quantity.”_
    *   Instead of pruning, update old posts or merge similar articles.
*   _“Don’t overthink crawl budget unless you’re a massive site.”_

### **What Should You Do?**

1.  **Audit Your Site**: Use tools like Google Search Console to find duplicates or low-performing pages.
2.  **Keep What’s Useful**: Don’t delete articles that still get traffic or backlinks.
3.  **Redirect or Update**: For removed pages, set up redirects. For outdated content, refresh it.

**TL;DR**:

*   **Small sites**: Don’t stress about crawl budget. Focus on fixing duplicates and improving content.
*   **Big sites**: Prune duplicates to help Googlebot crawl efficiently.
*   **Always prioritize quality over quantity**—Google rewards helpful content, not a perfectly trimmed sitemap.
