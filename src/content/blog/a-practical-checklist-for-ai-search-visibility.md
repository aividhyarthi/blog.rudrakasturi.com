Work through this checklist over the next week. Each item links to the article that explains the reasoning.

## The basics (do these first)

- [ ] **Your pages can be crawled.** Check robots.txt does not block Google or the AI crawlers you want, such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot. ([AI crawler basics](/how-to-see-and-control-ai-overviews-and-ai-mode-in-search-console/))
- [ ] **Your site is built properly:** clean HTML, JavaScript that search engines can read, fast pages, and no heaps of duplicate content. Google lists these as priorities in its AI guide.
- [ ] **Your local or shop data is current:** Google Business Profile and Merchant Center feeds, if they apply to you.

## The content

- [ ] **Each key page answers the main question near the top**, in direct language.
- [ ] **Each page adds something original:** your data, your experience, your examples. Google calls this "non-commodity content".
- [ ] **Follow-up questions are answered on the same page**, with clear headings. ([Query fan-out](/what-is-query-fan-out-and-how-should-it-change-how-you-write/))
- [ ] **You do not make a page for every tiny variation.** That risks Google's scaled content rules.

## Skip these

- [ ] **llms.txt.** Google says it is not needed. ([Details](/do-you-need-an-llms-txt-file-what-google-has-said/))
- [ ] **Special "AI markup".** Google says there is no special schema for AI features. Keep normal structured data for rich results.
- [ ] **Chopping content into tiny pieces for robots.**

## Beyond your own site

- [ ] **Get mentioned where AI looks:** roundup articles, helpful community threads, YouTube, and other publications. ([Reddit and AI citations](/reddit-and-ai-answers-what-the-citation-data-actually-says/))
- [ ] **Check your Bing presence**, since ChatGPT depends on it for some brands. ([Why each AI differs](/why-chatgpt-perplexity-and-google-cite-different-sources/))

## Measure

- [ ] **Look at the AI report in Search Console**, impressions only. ([How](/how-to-see-and-control-ai-overviews-and-ai-mode-in-search-console/))
- [ ] **Test 20 customer questions** monthly in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google. Note who is cited.
- [ ] **Compare before and after** for pages hit by AI Overviews. ([What the studies say](/are-ai-overviews-really-killing-clicks-what-the-studies-say/))

If you only do three things: let crawlers in, add one original insight to your best page, and start the monthly prompt test.
