Google Discover was never a reliable source. It was a bonus traffic pipe - erratic, unexplained, and dependent on what Google _felt_ like showing users.

But now, the bonus is ending.

**What’s Changing in Google Discover Soon?**

Here’s the new reality, backed by what we’re seeing live across media publishers and platforms:

### **Discover Delays Are Breaking Timeliness** (This is already happening)

Articles are showing up hours, sometimes days late. If you thought breaking news could get a Discover boost — think again. By the time it hits the feed, your users are already on Instagram or X.

### **Web Stories Will Be Dead Soon**

Zero visibility is coming.  
Discover is already slashing Web Story slots, and many major publishers are reporting **<5%** share of traffic now. If you built teams around Stories, sunset them, fast.

### **YouTube Shorts Will Replace Your Articles**

Google’s own product **YouTube** **Shorts** is now _replacing_ article and story slots in Discover.  
Why? Because Google **promised creators better monetization for Shorts**. And where do you think the screen space will come from? Right. Your article cards.

Expect Shorts to dominate news, entertainment, lifestyle, and even explainer categories in coming weeks. This roll is already live in US.

### **Instagram Is Eating Into Discover Time**

Reels are capturing the scroll-time Discover once owned. Your content isn’t just competing with other articles, it’s competing with dancing doctors and meme accounts.

### **AI Overview Is Coming to Discover Too**

Google is experimenting with **AI snippets in Discover**. These summarize the article _inside_ the card, removing any incentive to click. **CTR = Crushed.**

### **Reddit Threads Will Become Google’s New Content Farm**

Discover is now surfacing Reddit Q&As in clean, AI-summarized answer cards. That’s **AI Mode** taking over your curated content space.

Google trusts Reddit’s mess more than your newsroom’s finesse. Sit with that.

**Here’s What You Should Be Doing:**

*   **Invest in Shorts:** Not for vanity, but because that’s what Google is prioritizing and monetizing.
*   **Prepare for AI SEO:** Structure your content for AI Overviews. Clear, concise, well-sourced, with entities that LLMs can extract.
*   **Diversify Beyond Google:** Build loyalty via apps, newsletters, and community-led channels like WhatsApp, Telegram, and even Reddit.
*   **Use Discover as a Bonus - Not a Plan**: If it hits, great. If not, your traffic strategy shouldn’t collapse.

**Why you should care?**

Google isn’t killing your visibility. It’s just replacing **_your content_ with _its content_** - Shorts, AI answers, Reddit threads. Adapt or keep refreshing your Discover tab in denial.
