Google’s much-hyped **AI Mode** (part of its Search Generative Experience or SGE) has finally rolled out in India. But if you tried searching in **Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, or any other Indian language**, you were likely met with a blank screen and this message:

**“AI Mode is only available in English right now. Stay tuned for more language support.”**

![](https://rudrakasturi.wpcomstaging.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Screenshot-2025-06-26-at-11.07.59 AM-1-938x1024.webp)

### So What Does This Mean for Indian Language Publishers?

**1\. AI Mode ≠ Multilingual (Yet)**

Despite the India rollout, **AI Mode is still English-only**. That means:

*   No summaries for regional-language queries
*   No disruption to Indian-language SEO or news discovery
*   No immediate impact on vernacular content visibility

**2\. Evergreen & Long-Tail Content in Regional Languages Will Stay Stable**

If you’re ranking for:

*   Health tips in Hindi
*   Devotional content in Telugu
*   Government schemes in Marathi
*   Local news in Tamil

...then **your traffic won’t move much** because AI Mode simply doesn’t process or summarize these results yet.

**3\. Publishers Still Own Their Turf**

In regional language search, **traditional blue link SEO still dominates**:

*   Crawling and indexing are still handled by the old-school Googlebot
*   Rankings depend on structure, metadata, site trust, and backlinks
*   No AI summaries mean no threat (yet) from generative snippets stealing clicks

### What’s Likely to Change in the Future?

Google is **testing** regional-language support, but it’s complex:

*   Most Indian language content is not uniformly structured
*   Dialect and script variations make summarization risky
*   Fewer “trusted” sources available for grounding AI responses

**Expect gradual rollout**, starting with Hindi and government queries, possibly via Hinglish.

### What Should Publishers Do Now?

*   **Double down on clarity** – AI Mode (when it arrives) rewards clean markup, structured info, and FAQ-style formats
*   **Keep investing in classic SEO** – titles, schema, speed, E-E-A-T still drive discovery
*   **Don’t chase AI Mode** – for Indian languages, it’s not your competition yet

## Final Word

For now, **AI Mode is English-only in India**, and that gives regional publishers **a big window of opportunity**. Focus on quality content, technical hygiene, and user trust and you’ll stay ahead, even when the AI catches up.

**The future of Indian language search will be AI-assisted, not AI-taken at least for now.**
