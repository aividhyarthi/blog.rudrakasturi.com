Google is changing how Discover works. Starting September 2025, you will see **follow buttons, social media posts, and dedicated spaces for creators and publishers** right inside Discover.

## What’s New in Discover

1.  **Follow creators and publishers directly**  
    Google said: _“When you see content from a creator or publisher in Discover, you can tap to follow them right there. Once you do, you’ll find a dedicated space with more of their content.”_
2.  **Social posts in Discover feed**  
    Google confirmed: _“We’re expanding the types of content in Discover. In addition to articles and videos, you’ll now see social posts from platforms like YouTube Shorts, X and Instagram.”_
3.  **Preview before following**  
    According to Google: _“You’ll be able to preview the types of content a publisher or creator shares before deciding to follow them, helping you make informed choices.”_

## Why This Matters

For Users

For Creators & Publishers

More personalization: one feed with news, videos, and social posts.

Social posts and short videos gain visibility in Discover, not just in apps.

Easy follow option to stay updated.

A “dedicated space” means stronger brand recall and repeated impressions.

Mix of formats: richer, more engaging experience.

Cross-posting content across website, YouTube, Instagram, X becomes more valuable.

## What Publishers and Creators Should Do

*   **Diversify content formats**: articles, reels, posts, Shorts.
*   **Optimize branding**: clear names, recognizable logos, high-quality previews.
*   **Be consistent**: regular updates help build follower trust.
*   **Think beyond SEO**: social signals now influence visibility in Discover.

## The Bigger Picture

This update blurs the lines between **social media feeds and Google Discover**. It creates a hybrid model where Google wants to keep users engaged with a mix of traditional web content and real-time social updates.

For India and other markets, this means **regional creators on Instagram, YouTube Shorts, and X have a new distribution channel**. Publishers should now treat Discover as a place to grow direct audience relationships, not just a traffic source.

**TL;DR**:

*   Discover now has **follow buttons** and **dedicated spaces** for publishers/creators.
*   **Social media posts (YouTube Shorts, Instagram, X)** will appear alongside articles.
*   Google says these changes help people _“catch up with the creators and publishers you care about.”_
