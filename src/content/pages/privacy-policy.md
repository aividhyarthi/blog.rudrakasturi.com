*Last updated: 18 October 2026*

This Privacy Policy explains how Rudra Kasturi Inc. ("we", "us", "our") handles information when you visit **blog.rudrakasturi.com** (the "Site"). By using the Site you agree to the practices described here.

## Information we collect

**Information you give us.** If you email us, contact us through WhatsApp or LinkedIn, or subscribe to our newsletter, we receive the details you provide, such as your name, email address and message.

**Information collected automatically.** When you visit the Site, your browser and our hosting provider may log technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed, referring page and the date and time of the visit. This is used to keep the Site secure and working.

**Cookies and similar technologies.** The Site and our advertising and analytics partners may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies to remember preferences, measure traffic and show ads. You can control cookies in your browser settings.

## How we use information

- To operate, maintain and improve the Site and our content.
- To respond to your messages and enquiries.
- To send the newsletter, only if you subscribe. You can unsubscribe at any time.
- To measure how the Site is used and to display advertising.
- To protect the Site and comply with legal obligations.

## Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to show ads on the Site. Google and its partners, including third-party vendors, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to our Site and to other sites on the internet.

- You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com).
- You can learn more about how Google uses information from sites that use its services at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
- You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info).

Where required by law, such as in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, ads personalisation and cookie use depend on your consent.

## Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Site and the newsletter (for example hosting, email delivery and advertising partners), when required by law, or to protect our rights. Those providers may only use the information to perform services for us.

## Third-party links and embedded tools

The Site links to other websites and embeds some tools and content from third parties, such as videos and analysis tools. We do not control their privacy practices. Please read their policies before sharing information with them.

## Data retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act and the EU/UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email us at the address below.

## Children

The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.
